## 1. Executive summary

The dataset cannot support the currently drafted paper ("three-year photometric baseline") — imaging ends 2024-03-26 and AAVSO owns that space. It *can* support a strong ApJ paper led by the **2025 slitless-grism Hα series: 247 spectra on 60 nights at ~2-day cadence through T CrB's post-dip accretion recovery**, supported by a **403-frame external B6 calibrator series (θ CrB — a known Be/shell star ~8° from the target; see §3)**, calibrated 2023–2024 photometric anchors, and an archival flickering *upper-limit* table. **Equivalent width is the season-long observable; absolute Hα flux is calibratable only for Mar–Apr 2025**, the sole interval with contemporaneous calibrator coverage. Real flickering science comes from the **2026 restart data, not the archive** — the archival "long runs" dissolve on inspection into multi-filter snapshot sequences (§2 Rank 2, §3). Two immediate non-analysis actions gate everything: **restart T CrB observations at the October 2026 re-opening** (2026-08-17 update: Winer is closed for monsoon until October, so the request is submitted now to execute at re-opening, first in queue — see "Planned observations" below; the target is dark since 2025-06-24, 14 months into the eruption window) and **run the P0-3 detector & calibration campaign** (bit-depth/linearity measurement, filter-code forensics, acquisition of the missing mode-matched calibration frames) before any photometric number is trusted. Target: ApJ, "Accretion diagnostics of T CrB across the pre-eruption dip and recovery," with a spectroscopic-methods appendix; make zero eruption-date predictions.

## 3. Data assessment (verified numbers)

Canonical accounting uses the **`rawimage` tree only** (471 unique light frames **= 414 `T CrB` + 57 `t crb`** — record that decomposition so the accounting never forks again); the catalog's 862 T CrB light-frame rows include 236 `reduced/` and 155 consortium-mirror (`external`/`iowa`/`macalester`) duplicates. Memos quoting 862 or 626 counted duplicates; cite 471 unique.

- **Span:** 2023-05-12 → 2025-06-24, 86 distinct nights. **Zero T CrB frames since 2025-06-24** (catalog runs to 2026-07-03; confirmed also by coordinate-box query). 2026 season missed entirely.
- **Imaging (all 2023-05 → 2024-03-26, 224 frames, 26 nights):** R 121 (0.25–8 s), B 23 (16–32 s), I 9, G 9, plus **unidentified single-character filter codes**: `6` (20), `L` (14), `W` (8), `O` (7), `1` (7), `H` (6, presumed Hα, all single-epoch 2024-03-13 — **excluded from science regardless of P0-2 mapping**; filter table only). Header carries only the bare character (M1 verified in pixels/headers). 62 frames unusable for calibrated work until mapped.
- **There are no monitoring-grade runs in the archival imaging** (referee-verified; this kills the old Rank-2 framing). 2024-03-13 is **42 unique rawimage frames across 11 filter/mode combinations** — the "232 min" is wall-clock span of a multi-filter snapshot sequence; the longest same-filter run that night is 12 `L` frames spanning 2.2 min, and the B "run" is 6 frames over ~11 min. 2024-02-13 is **10 B StackPro frames at ~16-min cadence** over 162 min — it cannot resolve flickering. The 2023 R bursts are 13 nights of short sequences whose three largest (2023-05-20/21/22: 14, 18, 8 frames) are StackPro frames on exactly the cloud nights the ZMAG cut auto-kills. What survives the cut: ≤15-frame, ≤11-min snippets in three readout modes. **The paper quotes a per-night table (frames, span, mode), not a duration range.**
- **Spectroscopy (all of 2025, Feb 21 – Jun 24):** lrg 147 frames / 59 nights, hrg 100 / 54 nights; all 240 s, Mode0, 2×2 binning, none plate-solved, no ZMAG. Median cadence ~2 d over 124 d. **Header pointings are untrustworthy: 21/247 frames carry pointings >1° off** (some at RA 73°, Dec −28°). Frame identity must be established per frame (Phase A.0), never assumed.
- **Calibrator: θ CrB (HD 138749, B6Vnn, V≈4.1) — a documented classical Be star with recurrent shell episodes and long-term variable Hα emission.** It is **not** a clean-absorption standard at Hα, and it sits **~8° from T CrB** — every observation is a separate pointing at different airmass/flexure, so it is an *external* spectrophotometric standard, never "same-field." Grism series: **403 unique rawimage frames, 40 nights, 2025-03-10 → 2026-06-02**, heterogeneous: 2025 frames at 0.085–4.9 s Mode0; 2026 frames at 60 s in Mode0 plus a fourth readout mode, **"Fast" (55 frames)**. Its header RA spans 13.7°–233.3° — the identity gate applies to it too. **Within the T CrB season the calibrator exists only 2025-03-10 → 2025-04-17 (21 nights); same-night θ CrB exists on 17 of 60 T CrB grism nights; all of Feb (3 nights), May (15), and Jun (14) 2025 — 32/60 nights, the entire late recovery — have zero contemporaneous calibrator.** No calibrator frame ever matches the T CrB configuration (240 s Mode0).
- **Readout modes mixed:** High Gain (EGAIN≈1.05 e⁻/ADU), High Gain StackPro (on-camera co-added reads, **non-Poisson correlated noise, undocumented**), Low Gain (22.6 e⁻/ADU), Mode0, plus θ CrB's "Fast". R-band alone spans three modes. Never share an *error model* across modes; the master-calibration rule is amended by the inventory below (ruling 7).
- **Photometric health:** 311/379 imaging rows (incl. reduced tree) plate-solved with ZMAG. R-band ZMAG mean 21.30, full range 18.35–24.16; within-night spreads up to 3.6 mag on 2023-05-20/21/22/27 (clouds). Good nights: 0.04–0.05 mag. Median FWHM 4.1 px ≈ 2.2″; airmass mean 1.13, max 2.5.
- **Calibration inventory (verified — the rule meets reality):** darks exist for High Gain (12 dates), StackPro (8), Low Gain (2), 1MHz (3); **zero Mode0 darks or biases** — the mode of all 247 spectra, at 240 s where CMOS dark structure/amp glow is largest and sits directly under the dispersed-background bands. Flats exist **only in High Gain** (+1MHz/5MHz): every StackPro and Low Gain science frame — including all 2023 StackPro bursts and the 2024-02-13 B sequence — has **no mode-matched flat, ever**. Both R-flat epochs (2023-07-01, 2024-01-15) fall outside the imaging seasons. No fringe frames for these filters. Consequences and the amended rule: ruling 7 and Phase B.1.
- **Target status (M4):** no eruption as of Aug 2026; recovered from the 2023–24 dip; Schaefer's 2025.5±1.3 window nearly exhausted; next proposed window Feb 2027. **Every target-status statement carries a verify-at-submission flag**, and the weekly sweep covers arXiv **plus ATels and AAVSO alerts** (which move faster than arXiv for this object).

### Chair's rulings on inter-seat disagreements

1. **Frame counts (414 vs 471 vs 626 vs 862):** resolved above — 471 unique rawimage frames = 414 + 57 (case split); README's 414/83 is stale; update the count *and* its framing sentence (§9).
2. **Saturation/linearity (M1 vs M2 — the big one).** M1 read the same 8 s R frame (peak 3565 ADU) as ~5% of a 65 535 ADU ceiling (safe, SNR≈600); M2 read it as ~87% of a ~4095 ADU High-Gain ceiling (deep nonlinearity), noting 3565 was the frame's global maximum. Both cannot be right, and the answer decides whether 121 R frames are usable. **Ruling: neither interpretation is adopted on faith — the ceiling must be measured.** P0-3's **first task is the one-afternoon hardware check that arbitrates directly: is this 4k×4k CMOS's High Gain channel natively 12-bit with ADU rescaling in the headers?** Then exposure-ladder turnover fits per readout mode (θ CrB/field stars plus the archive's 0.25–8 s R and 0.25/1/4 s I ladders on T CrB). Until then every photometric number from 8 s R frames is provisional. **Branch plan, stated now (the referee's M6):** under M2's 12-bit hypothesis the 1 s R frames (peak ~3.3 kADU ≈ 80% of a 4095 ceiling) *also fail* the 70% cap — there is no "safe fallback" R series. If M2 is right, **no archival R frame of T CrB survives the cap**: the 2023–2024 anchors carry on **B and I** plus whatever P0-2 rescues from the unmapped codes, and a nonlinearity-*corrected* (rather than capped) R series is admissible only with an explicitly inflated systematic term tabulated separately. If M1 is right, R proceeds capped at 70% of the measured ceiling. Regardless of outcome: wide apertures (r≈3×FWHM) and FWHM as a detrending covariate, because seeing-modulated clipping mimics flickering (M2's key warning).
3. **Comparison-star source (M1: ATLAS-REFCAT2 ensemble vs M3: AAVSO sequence):** use **REFCAT2 for the ensemble solution** (homogeneous, dense, color info for the color-term fit) and the **AAVSO sequence stars as the cross-validation set** reported in the paper (referees know them). Both live in the 37′ field; no conflict in practice.
4. **ZMAG cut threshold (M1: >0.3 mag below clear-sky mode; M2: >0.5 below season median; M3: >1.0 below nightly envelope):** adopt **0.5 mag below the per-filter season mode** as the default cut, with the M1 0.3 mag flag recorded per frame so the sensitivity of results to the threshold can be tabulated (one sentence + one table column in the paper).
5. **Scope of the time-series analysis:** M2's restriction stands and is tightened — per-season short-period search only with **upper period bound = season span / 3** (≈8 d for the 23 d 2023 season, ≈14 d for the 42 d 2024 season; the old 20 d bound gave <1.2 cycles at the long end and would only advertise emptiness). Spectral window published alongside every periodogram, bootstrap FAPs, injection-recovery contours, ±1 cyc/d alias rule. No orbital/ellipsoidal fits (rank 5 above). M3's request to "measure the ellipsoidal amplitude per season" stays downgraded to *mean level per season phase-anchored on the literature ephemeris* — means only, no amplitude language.
6. **Eclipse timing / O−C:** dropped entirely (M2). No timable events exist.
7. **Cross-mode calibration (new — forced by the verified inventory).** The absolute rule "never share a master calibration across modes" is unimplementable as written: StackPro and Low Gain photometry have no mode-matched flats, and Mode0 spectroscopy has no darks or biases at all. Amended rule: (a) **acquire now** — Mode0 240 s darks/biases and current-configuration flats in every active mode, as facility-level products under P0-3/`RLMT_Diagnostics` (the telescope is idle for this target anyway); (b) for archival frames, either demonstrate empirically that the flanking-band background subtraction removes dark structure to below the θ CrB-derived floor (test shown in Appendix B), or apply a cross-mode calibration **with a measured penalty term** carried in the systematic budget. The contradiction between §3's old rule and the data's reality is resolved here, in the plan — not left for a referee to find.

## 4. Analysis method — step-by-step

Environment: `/opt/miniconda3/envs/rlmt-checks/bin/python` (astropy 6.1.7). Add: `photutils`, `specreduce`, `specutils`, `celerite2`, `emcee`, `astroquery`. All code in a `pipeline/` dir under this project; every product regenerable from `rlmt-catalog.sqlite` + `rawimage/`.

### Phase 0 — Gates (week 1; nothing downstream starts until done)

- **P0-1 Restart observations.** CrB is up in evening twilight through ~October. Nightly: 3×1 s r + 3×short B; one lrg + one hrg (240 s each). **Weekly: one ≥2 hr B flickering run at ≤60 s cadence — this is now the *only* path to flickering science (Rank 2b); the archive cannot supply it.** Load the eruption-response block into pyscope now (§6).
- **P0-2 Filter forensics.** Map codes `6,1,G,H,L,O,W` to physical bandpasses via observer logs, `observer`/`fn_user` columns, pyscope/MaxIm filter-wheel configs of the relevant epochs, and headers of adjacent calibration frames. Deliverable: a filter-mapping table (goes in the paper). Unmapped frames are excluded from calibrated products — usable only as labeled instrumental series. The 6 `H` frames are single-epoch (2024-03-13) and stay excluded from science regardless of mapping.
- **P0-3 Detector & calibration campaign** (resolves rulings 2 and 7). In order: (i) the **High Gain bit-depth hardware check** (12-bit with header ADU rescaling vs true 16-bit — headers/driver docs plus a saturated-star afternoon test; arbitrates M1 vs M2 directly); (ii) exposure ladders per readout mode, fit the turnover, adopt hard cap = 70% of measured ceiling for target *and* ensemble stars; (iii) **acquire Mode0 240 s darks + biases and flats in every active mode** (facility-level products, documented in `RLMT_Diagnostics`); (iv) **shutter/exposure-timing uniformity at short exposures** — the eruption plan calls for 0.1 s, and the archive's 0.085 s θ CrB frames are the ready-made test set; (v) ZMAG provenance (which catalog bandpass PinPoint solved against, per filter).
- **P0-4 Re-solve astrometry.** astrometry.net `solve-field` (4100-series indices) on all unsolved imaging frames (~40% lack WCS). Grism frames get the Phase A.0 identity gate instead of WCS.

### Planned observations (October 2026 restart) — 2026-08-17 update

Winer Observatory is offline for monsoon season until **October 2026**; P0-1's "restart this month" is physically impossible and is superseded as follows. The consolidated request is **submitted now, to execute at the October re-opening, first in queue** (`ops/2026-08_observatory_request.md`).

- **From first light at re-opening:** the P0-1 nightly block — 3×1 s r + short B + one 240 s lrg + one 240 s hrg grism pair (continuing the 2025 Hα EW series homogeneously).
- **Weekly ≥2 hr B flickering runs at ≤60 s cadence, resuming at the October re-opening.** Visibility honesty (P0-1's own note: CrB is up in evening twilight through ~October): at best a brief twilight window follows re-opening before solar conjunction, and no ≥2 hr dark-sky run is possible until the field re-emerges — the runs accrue mainly in the 2027 observing season. The ≥6-good-runs threshold (Rank 2b, Phase C.2) is unchanged; the contingent flickering subsection's timeline shifts with the visibility — the "2026 restart data" becomes the 2026–27 season, weighted toward 2027.
- **Eruption-response pyscope block: loaded and verified during the closure, before re-opening** (software-side; no dome needed). Unchanged in content (§6.5).
- **Monsoon-gap coverage (Aug–Oct):** the Phase D external pulls — AAVSO AID, ASAS-SN, ZTF/ATLAS-class survey photometry, ARAS spectra — cover the gap and are fetched **now**, not in October. If T CrB erupts during the monsoon, the eruption letter pivots to external data and RLMT joins post-restart.
- **Pipeline ingest:** October frames are first-class planned inputs. After each post-October archive sync, re-run S0 (manifest) then S0b (calibration/linkage inventory) — both idempotent — and diff the manifest; the new nights enter this project's tables through that loop, never by hand.

0. **Identity gate (new — non-negotiable).** No grism frame is plate-solved and header pointings are demonstrably unreliable (21/247 T CrB frames >1° off; θ CrB headers span RA 13.7°–233.3°). Per frame: match the zero-order image's field-star pattern against Gaia DR3 by relative geometry (no WCS needed — relative geometry suffices); confirm the target's zero-order position; flag and discard failures. **Report the discard fraction in the paper** alongside the saturation triage. Applies to the θ CrB series too. Nothing downstream in Phase A uses a frame that has not passed this gate.
1. **Calibrator characterization before calibrator use.** θ CrB is a Be/shell star (§3). First pipeline task on it: measure its own Hα (and Hβ) profile and its stability across all 403 frames, split by (exposure, mode, epoch). If emission/shell features are present at any level during 2025–2026, that spectral region is astrophysics, not instrument, and is excluded from wavelength anchoring and response derivation below. The paper states θ CrB's Be nature regardless of the outcome.
2. **Extraction** (`specreduce`): per-frame trace of the first-order spectrum, background from dispersed-sky bands flanking the trace (model field-star dispersed contamination per frame — slitless!), boxcar and optimal extraction, keep both. The flanking-band subtraction doubles as the Mode0 dark-structure remover (ruling 7); its adequacy is *demonstrated* in Appendix B, not assumed.
3. **Wavelength solution per frame, self-anchored:** zero point from the zero-order centroid geometry plus T CrB's own Hα; dispersion from the grism model, tracked night-to-night, separately for lrg and hrg. θ CrB Balmer lines (**Hβ/Hγ — not Hα**, which may be disk-contaminated per A.1) serve as *validation* on the 17 same-night nights, never as the solution — they are undefined on the other 43. Never a global solution. In slitless spectroscopy the wavelength zero point is degenerate with source position: **every hrg velocity carries the per-frame zero-order-referenced zero-point uncertainty in km s⁻¹ beside it, or is reported only as a differential (night-to-night) velocity change.**
4. **Instrument response** from θ CrB against a CALSPEC-adjacent B6 template, **with the Hα region interpolated over** (Be contamination, A.1): same-night ratio on the 17 matched nights only. **Absolute Hα flux is therefore reported for Mar–Apr 2025 only** (the 2025-03-10 → 2025-04-17 calibrator window); Feb, May, and Jun 2025 have zero contemporaneous calibrator and get **no absolute fluxes — EW only**, stated plainly in the paper (extrapolating a 2026 response taken at different exposures and partly in "Fast" mode across 2+ months is not calibration). θ CrB is an external standard ~8° away: fit and report an airmass-dependent response term, and state which frames' airmass ranges overlap. The 2026 θ CrB frames (60 s, Mode0/"Fast") are analyzed as a *separate configuration*, useful for long-term response-*stability* claims only — never pooled into the 2025 floor.
5. **Primary observable: Hα equivalent width** — robust to gray slit losses, defined season-long. Fix integration window and pseudo-continuum bands to **Munari's convention** so the series splices onto published curves; the M4III TiO bands make the continuum treacherous — document windows explicitly, test sensitivity to ±10 Å shifts. Secondary: Hα absolute flux (Mar–Apr only, per A.4); profile morphology (absorption reversal) and *differential* wing-velocity evolution from hrg (per A.3).
6. **Saturation triage:** scripted astropy pass over every grism frame; flag zero-order and Hα-peak pixels > 80% of the measured ceiling inside the extraction aperture; discard flagged frames and report the fraction.
7. **Error floor, rebuilt:** night-to-night EW scatter of θ CrB **from the matched 2025 Mode0 subset only, split by (exposure, mode)** — no pooling across the heterogeneous series. Because Be variability could masquerade as instrument noise, the floor is cross-checked against (a) repeatability in line-free continuum windows and (b) a genuinely inert field star in the θ CrB frames if one is bright enough. **No calibrator frame samples the 240 s regime**: flexure/tracking smear over 240 s changes the spectral PSF in ways a ≤5 s calibrator frame cannot see — quantify it from T CrB's own frame-to-frame trace widths and quote it as a separate floor term. This, with the Be caveat, is the methods appendix.
8. **Cross-validation:** EW overlap vs ARAS database spectra and any published 2025 EW points — demand agreement within 10–15% or explain. Same task delivers the Rank-1 "densest record" table: spectra per month, RLMT vs ARAS vs Asiago/Munari, Feb–Jun 2025; if ARAS wins any month, the intro claim carries the "homogeneous single-instrument" qualifier explicitly.

### Phase B — Photometry (supporting)

1. **Calibrate per readout mode, honestly (ruling 7):** mode-matched master darks/bias where they exist (High Gain, StackPro, Low Gain darks). **StackPro and Low Gain have no mode-matched flats and never will for the archival epochs** — apply the High Gain flat with a **measured cross-mode penalty term** (flat-field structure difference bounded via ensemble residual vs detector position), carried in the systematic budget. Both R-flat epochs are out-of-season; quantify staleness the same way. New-season frames use the P0-3(iii) calibration set.
2. **Aperture photometry** (`photutils`), not PSF (bright isolated target, unstable PSF, no analytic noise model). Per-frame r = 1.5×FWHM with curve-of-growth correction, cross-checked against fixed 8 px and wide 3×FWHM apertures; adopt whichever minimizes check-star scatter *and* passes the linearity comparison (wide aperture dilutes core nonlinearity).
3. **Ensemble differential calibration:** 15–30 REFCAT2 stars (10.5 < r < 13.5, isolated >10″, 0.4 < g−r < 1.4); robust (Huber) per-frame ZP + color term; iterative variable rejection. Extrapolate the color term to T CrB's M4III color and **propagate the extrapolation uncertainty explicitly — T CrB (g−r ≳ 1.5, TiO-dominated) sits outside the ensemble fit range, worst in B**; include second-order extinction k″·(color)·X (airmass to 2.5). Report natural-system and transformed (R→r, B→g) magnitudes with the transformation uncertainty separated.
4. **ZMAG demoted to QC only** (ruling 4): cut frames >0.5 mag below the per-filter season mode. Never use ZMAG as calibration.
5. **Empirical errors:** per-frame uncertainty = RMS of 3–5 check stars bracketing T CrB's magnitude, per night per mode. No Poisson arithmetic on StackPro, ever. Merge modes only after overlap nights agree to <10 mmag.
6. **Precision target (M1's budget, adopted):** 5–8 mmag per frame, 3–5 mmag nightly means, 5–10 mmag season-level systematic floor. Do not promise mmag-class work.

### Phase C — Time series

1. **Archival flickering = per-snippet table, nothing more (referee M1).** There are no archival monitoring runs (§3): for every surviving post-ZMAG-cut snippet (≤15 frames, ≤11 min, mode-labeled), report a detrended rms with check-star comparison (joint linear detrend in airmass, FWHM, x/y, ZMAG) and a **95% upper limit on σ_flick** from injecting red-noise (β≈1–2) flickering into that snippet's actual comp-star residuals — an honest statement of what an 11-minute StackPro snippet can bound. **No 2023-vs-2024 state comparison; no PSDs from archival data** (the longest same-filter run is 12 frames / 2.2 min). Compare limits to Zamanov benchmarks (~0.1–0.2 mag B) as fractional rms where bands differ, labeled as limits.
2. **2026 flickering runs (contingent on P0-1):** for each weekly ≥2 hr run, fit a celerite2 SHO/Matérn-3/2 GP *simultaneously* with the flickering PSD model (never subtract-then-search), kernel timescale bounded above 2× run length, joint detrend covariates as above. Report rms, structure function, per-run PSD (a 2 hr run at 60 s cadence reaches ~0.14–8 mHz). This subsection exists in the paper only if ≥6 good runs land; otherwise Rank 2 is the archival limits table alone.
3. **Per-season period search** (P from 1.5 d to **span/3**: ≈8 d in 2023, ≈14 d in 2024 — ruling 5) on nightly means: astropy `LombScargle` + PDM; bootstrap FAP (10⁴ resamples of real timestamps); accept a peak only if it beats its ±1 cyc/d aliases by Δln(FAP)>2 (47/85 night gaps are exactly 1 d). Injection-recovery over (P, A) grid; quote the 90%-recovery contour (~8–10 mmag floor expected).
4. **Uncertainties on quoted numbers:** emcee posteriors; red-noise inflation factor from binned comp-star rms; value ± stat ± sys with the linearity and cross-mode penalty terms listed separately.

## 5. Calibration & validation plan

| Check | Method | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|
| High Gain bit depth | P0-3(i) hardware check (headers/driver + saturated-star test) | 12-bit vs 16-bit settled before any R number is quoted; ruling-2 branch invoked as measured |
| Detector linearity | Ladder fit per mode (P0-3) | Ceiling measured; all photometry capped at 70% |
| Grism frame identity | Phase A.0 per-frame Gaia field-pattern match on the zero-order image | Target confirmed or frame discarded; discard fraction published |
| θ CrB astrophysical stability | Hα/Hβ profile monitoring across all 403 frames (A.1) | Be/shell activity characterized; Hα excluded from response and wavelength anchoring if present |
| Mode0 dark structure | Flanking-band subtraction adequacy test (Appendix B) | Residual dark structure < θ CrB-derived floor, or a penalty term carried |
| Cross-mode flats | Ensemble residual vs (x,y) with High Gain flat on StackPro/Low Gain frames | Penalty term measured and carried in the sys budget (ruling 7) |
| StackPro noise | Empirical check-star RMS vs magnitude; Allan deviation | Data sit on empirical floor; no Poisson bars anywhere |
| Mode merging | High Gain vs StackPro vs Low Gain overlap nights (R) | <10 mmag agreement before merging |
| Flat staleness | Ensemble residual vs (x,y); star-position scatter | Bounded, folded into systematic floor |

## 6. Failure modes & mitigations

1. **The coverage cliff (top risk).** No T CrB data since 2025-06-24, 14 months of the eruption window dark. *Mitigation:* restart at the October re-opening, first in queue (P0-1 as amended by the 2026-08-17 "Planned observations" update; external data bridge the monsoon gap); if resumption fails, retitle honestly as an archival 2025-recovery study. A "monitoring" paper that stopped monitoring is a standing referee invitation.
2. **Calibrator coverage gap (new — second risk).** 32/60 grism nights — the entire late recovery — have zero contemporaneous θ CrB, and no calibrator frame ever matches the 240 s Mode0 science configuration. *Mitigation:* absolute flux restricted to Mar–Apr 2025, EW-only elsewhere (Phase A.4, built into the results plan, not discovered later); coverage figure (Fig 1) shows the calibrator band so the gap is disclosed on sight.
3. **Calibrator astrophysics (new).** θ CrB is a Be/shell star; its "stability" may be its variability. *Mitigation:* Phase A.1 characterization first; Hα excluded from anchoring/response; error floor cross-checked on line-free windows and an inert field star; Be nature stated in the paper regardless.
4. **Frame-identity failures (new).** Untrustworthy grism pointings (21/247 frames >1° off). *Mitigation:* Phase A.0 Gaia identity gate per frame; discard fraction published.
5. **Eruption during analysis.** *Mitigation — pre-loaded response plan (M3, adopted):* trigger = AAVSO alert or any point ≥1.0 mag above quiescent model confirmed ≥10 min later. Bright phase (V≈2–6): do **not** attempt direct imaging (saturates at any exposure) — grisms are the attenuator: lrg+hrg 0.1–5 s ladders at hours cadence (short-exposure timing accuracy verified in P0-3 iv first). Decline (V≳7): nightly short-exposure r/B; dense coverage of the ~100-day secondary maximum (unexplained 1946 signature — guaranteed paper section). Pyscope high-priority block configured this month.
6. **Nonlinearity masquerading as flickering** (seeing→peak→clipping correlates with FWHM exactly like real flickering). *Mitigation:* P0-3 bit-depth + ceiling measurement, 70% cap, wide-aperture cross-check, FWHM covariate — all non-negotiable; ruling-2 branch plan pre-commits the response if R dies.
7. **Unidentified filters** (62 rawimage frames). *Mitigation:* P0-2 forensics; unmapped ⇒ excluded from calibrated products; `H` excluded regardless (single-epoch); filter table in paper.
8. **Missing mode-matched calibrations** (no Mode0 darks; no StackPro/Low Gain flats). *Mitigation:* ruling 7 — acquire now for current modes (P0-3 iii); measured penalty terms for archival frames; Appendix B adequacy test for the Mode0 background.
9. **StackPro/Mode0 noise model unknown.** *Mitigation:* empirical errors only; mode-segregated error models; state it in Methods.
10. **Scooping** (Munari-adjacent groups publish continuously; 2026 A&A multi-λ paper already covers the re-brightening). *Mitigation:* weekly sweep of arXiv **plus ATels and AAVSO alerts**; our defensible niche is cadence + homogeneity + independent validation, claimed only as far as the Rank-1 spectra-per-month table supports it. All target-status sentences carry a verify-at-submission flag.
11. **Slitless systematics** (second-order overlap, dispersed field stars, gray slit losses, position–wavelength degeneracy). *Mitigation:* per-frame background modeling; EW as primary observable; per-frame zero-point uncertainty quoted with every velocity (A.3); θ CrB repeatability (rebuilt, A.7) as the error floor.
12. **Prediction temptation.** Any forecast sentence draws blood given the field's record. Report diagnostics; cite Schaefer 2023/RNAAS 2024 and arXiv:2607.05200 for context; forecast nothing.
13. **Cloud nights poisoning means.** ZMAG cut (ruling 4) + per-frame flags.
