**Subject:** Your 2023 RLMT dwarf-candidate imaging — filter curves, target list, and authorship

---

Dear John,

I have been working through the RLMT archive as a whole (2023–2026, about 330,000 frames)
to see which programmes can be turned into papers, and one of them is yours: the
spring–summer 2023 imaging of the dwarf-galaxy candidate fields and NGC 5238, taken with
the SBIG AC4040 in L, R and Hα. I should have written to you before going any further
with it, and I am doing so now, before anything is drafted.

Three things, in order of how much they hold up.

**1. Filter transmission curves.** I cannot turn an Hα image into a flux, or even say
which recession velocities a non-detection excludes, without the bandpass of the Hα
filter that was in the wheel in 2023 (centre and width at minimum; a measured curve if one
exists), and ideally the same for the [S II], [O III], and continuum filters. Do you have
the manufacturer's curves or the purchase record? If the answer is "ask someone else",
a name would be just as useful. Two related questions, if you happen to know:
what element sat in wheel slot "6" during 2023 (our pixel evidence says a grism, on
every night), and whether any flats were ever taken through slot "W" beyond the single
frame of 2023-07-01.

**2. Your target dossier.** The 19 field centres match the Karachentsev et al. DESI
Legacy Local Volume candidates, with one exception (Dw1643+07, which lies outside that
footprint). Could you share the target list as you built it — where each candidate came
from, what was already known about it, and what you were hoping the Hα images would show?
I would rather start from your reasoning than reconstruct it, and I do not want to
"discover" something you have already followed up elsewhere.

**3. Authorship, and whether you want this done at all.** These are your programme's
data. If there is a paper in them, I think it is yours to lead or to co-lead, and at the
least you and your student observers belong on it. If you have work in progress on these
fields, tell me and I will stay out of its way. I am taking a draft authorship policy to
the consortium (attached / linked) that says exactly this about PI programmes, and I
would value your reaction to it.

For honesty about what the data can support: a review of the plan last week concluded
that the Hα frames are probably read-noise limited at the exposures used, that no flat
field for the AC4040 can be taken any more (the camera left the telescope in March 2024),
and that the NGC 5548 "monitoring" frames from the same season are slitless spectra, not
images, so that part is gone. What remains is detection or non-detection of Hα in each
candidate, stated as a velocity range, plus broadband depth — worth a short paper if the
candidates are not already classified, and not worth one if they are. Your dossier
decides which.

A reply of any length would help; a twenty-minute call would be better. Thank you.

Best,
James
