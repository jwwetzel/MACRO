## 1. Executive summary

One ApJ paper: **"Hα and deep optical imaging of Local Volume dwarf-galaxy candidates and the interacting dwarf NGC 5238, with photometric monitoring of NGC 5548, from a 0.5 m telescope."** The lead science is Hα detection/upper limits for the **13 of 19** published DESI-Legacy dwarf-candidate fields that have Hα imaging (novelty to be certified against the Kaisin & Karachentsev Hα program and DESI spectroscopy in Phase 3.1), plus deep-imaging vetting **restricted to candidates the per-field injection–recovery limits can actually reach** (Phase 3.4). NGC 5238 (21 nights, 100 d, multiband, from the same era as the imaging analyzed by the 2024 tidal-tail papers) is the second pillar. NGC 5548 is a calibrated **band-integrated** nightly light curve — the adopted band likely contains reverberating BLR lines (Phase 4.5) — intercalibrated with ZTF/ASAS-SN/ATLAS and overlaid on the Xi et al. 2025 RM season, **conditional on a coverage/precision table demonstrating a real densification/precision gain**; otherwise the pre-named RNAAS fallback executes. Explicitly **not** a lag or period measurement. Everything is gated on the Phase 0 hygiene tasks: frame deduplication **with pointing validation**, filter-slot identification (science *and* calibration frames), uniform plate-solving, and an empirical StackPro noise model.

All counts below are **unique frames = distinct JD per target/filter** (verified 2026-08-16). **Mispointing is the second-order data fact:** unique-JD counting alone missed a wholly mispointed night (below); Phase 0.1 therefore validates pointing per frame, and no count is quotable until Phase 0.3 plate-solving closes.

## 4. Analysis method — step by step

**Environment:** conda env `rlmt-checks` (`/opt/miniconda3/envs/rlmt-checks/bin/python`, astropy 6.1.7). Add: photutils, sep, astrometry.net (solve-field + 4100-series indexes), reproject or SWarp, imfit, celerite2, emcee, GaiaXPy, calviacat (or hand-rolled equivalent), noisechisel or SExtractor, HOTPANTS or a ZOGY implementation, PyCALI/PyROA.

### Phase 0 — Gate tasks (nothing else starts until these finish)

0.1 **Deduplicate + validate pointing.** Build the master frame list from the catalog with `GROUP BY target_best, filter, jd` (tie-break by tree preference macalester > rawimage). Duplicate screening: JD collision + file size is the criterion at 329k-file scale; MD5 of decompressed pixels on a *sample* of suspicious pairs validates the criterion for the paper's dedup claim. Add two pointing-validation columns: (a) header RA/Dec vs target coordinates; (b) after Phase 0.3, solved-WCS center vs target. Emit `frames_unique.csv` with per-target/filter/night counts and per-frame disposition — this table is the paper's frame-disposition source and the denominator for every uncertainty. **No frame count enters the manuscript until 0.3 closes.** The NGC 5548 night 2023-03-25 tracking failure (§3) is the type specimen; Appendix C reports what those frames actually recorded.
0.2 **Resolve the filter-slot map — science *and* calibration frames, per epoch** ('6' for NGC 5548; G/O/W/X for NGC 5238; **and H/L/R for the Dw survey — the same wheel-letter scheme gets no free pass**): (a) ask Cannon / consortium config logs for spring-2023 wheel configs **and the Hα filter transmission curve (center, width)**; (b) independently, regress instrumental − catalog magnitudes of field stars against catalog (g−r) using ATLAS-REFCAT2 and Gaia DR3 XP synthetic photometry for candidate bandpasses (V, R, L, Sloan r…) — the bandpass minimizing color-term residual scatter wins; (c) cross-check with synthetic photometry against the 2024-06-05 g/r/i visit. For each *flat epoch*, establish which physical filter each slot label held at that date before any cross-epoch flat is applied. Report the adopted bands and color coefficients in the paper. Both (a) and (b) are required; they must agree.
0.3 **Plate-solve everything** with local astrometry.net, SIP order 2 — recovers the ~250 unsolved Dw rows and gives NGC 5548 WCS/FWHM/ZP for the first time. Check residual curvature vs field radius across the 37′ field before committing to SIP-2 and the <0.2″ rms claim; escalate order if radial structure appears. Frames that still fail: one visual triage pass, then an explicit "excluded, N=…" line. **Deliverable feeding Phase 1.1 and 4.1: the NGC 5548 per-frame pointing-jitter distribution and FWHM history.**
0.4 **StackPro/High Gain noise model (appendix material).** Photon-transfer curves from frame pairs, **stated concretely:** the 2023-06-07/08 repeated darks (32/64/128 s, ≥10 frames in each mode) provide dark/bias PTC pairs per mode; illuminated pairs come from `cmos_tests/` *only where readout mode is verified to match* — the 2023-07-01 twilights (1–2 frames/filter, 0.1–2.3 s, gradient-prone, mode unverified) are not assumed usable. **If no matched-mode illuminated pairs exist, the StackPro gain model degrades to dark-based + star-based, and the appendix says so.** Check the 256 s darks for CMOS amp glow in the corners. Deliverables: effective gain, effective read noise per product frame, and the ADU level where variance departs >2% from linear — that level (not 65,535) is the working saturation ceiling. Star-based linearity check on one photometric NGC 5548 night (regress instrumental vs REFCAT2 over 10 < m < 16; look for a bright-end bend); verify the AGN aperture and every ensemble star sit below the measured ceiling. Propagate measured gain/RN into photutils error arrays.

### Phase 1 — Calibration frames

1.1 **Flat strategy, per campaign per filter — an executable path stated for every band used:**
  - **Dw L/R/Hα and NGC 5238 slots with 2024-01-15 coverage:** delta sky flats from the dithered science frames (dithering verified, ~86″×61″): aggressive source masking (noisechisel/SExtractor segmentation grown 2×FWHM), nightly medians of ~100+ masked 256 s frames, normalize, smooth ~200 px; apply on top of the 2024-01-15 archival flat (which supplies high-frequency pixel structure), *after* the Phase 0.2 per-epoch slot mapping confirms which physical filter that flat is. Difference delta vs archival and **publish the residual map** (expect ≲1% high-frequency; target <0.2% of sky large-scale ≈ μ≈28 systematics floor).
  - **NGC 5548 slot '6' — no archival flat exists at any epoch.** Decision tree, driven by the Phase 0.3 jitter measurement: (i) if the campaign is dithered enough for source-free coverage per pixel, build a pure delta sky flat from the 133 science frames; (ii) if pointing clusters tightly (as header pointings suggest), **do not fabricate a flat — bound the systematic instead**: for near-fixed-pixel differential photometry, the flat-field error enters only as (pointing jitter) × (local flat gradient); estimate the gradient scale from the L-band archival flat as a structural proxy, compute the mmag bound from the measured jitter, and carry it as an explicit line in the NGC 5548 error budget. Small-dither differential photometry is far more forgiving than LSB work — but the bound is *computed*, not asserted.
  - **NGC 5238 slot 'W' — single 0.4 s flat (2023-07-01) only.** Either recover consortium W flats from outside this archive (ask Cannon), or **drop W from all surface-photometry claims**; W frames then serve detection/QC only, stated in the Data section.
1.2 **Fringing and moonlight gradients: test, don't assume.** Median-combine each field's masked unregistered frames per filter; if periodic structure >0.2% of sky appears (most likely Hα, I-band), build a 2023-era fringe map from the survey frames with the consortium machinery; else state "<0.2%" and move on. The 2024 i/y/z `fringes/` library does not apply. **Hα frames span 22–87% moon illumination:** fit and subtract a low-order (plane/quadratic) sky gradient per frame before stacking; if residual gradients correlate with moon angle/phase, build per-moon-regime delta flats.

### Phase 2 — Photometric calibration (shared across all targets)

2.1 **Per-image catalog ZMAG is QC only, never science calibration** (PinPoint fit, unmodeled color terms, absent for all NGC 5548 frames).
2.2 **Ensemble zero points:** calviacat-style least squares against **ATLAS-REFCAT2** (primary; all-sky, PS1-tied, clean at 11–16 mag), fitting ZP + linear color term per filter per readout-mode epoch, 2.5σ clipping; PS1 direct as cross-check. Campaign-level first-order extinction (all X < 1.5; NGC 5548 mean 1.07); second-order negligible for color-matched ensembles at ΔX < 0.5.
2.3 **L and slot filters never appear as bare magnitudes.** Publish transformations (L ≈ r + c·(g−r) or Gaia-G tie) with scatter folded into errors; anything absolute leans on R. **All depth and SB limits are quoted in the identified band — never as "L."**
⚖ Memo 1 favored REFCAT2, Memo 3 PS1. **Call: REFCAT2 primary** (uniform all-sky, right dynamic range), PS1 as the independent check — they are tied systems, so this costs nothing.

### Phase 3 — Dwarf fields (Q1, Q2)

3.1 **Cross-match first — and it fixes the counts:** 19 field centers vs Karachentsev+2022/2025 tables (VizieR/LVGDB); resolve **Dw1643+07's provenance explicitly** (RA 250.70°, Dec +7.67° — outside the survey footprint; possibly not a Karachentsev object; Cannon will know). Per candidate, record: (a) ELVES-Field 2026 (arXiv:2602.16778) / HSC (arXiv:2504.11608) confirmation status; (b) **existing Hα imaging — the Kaisin & Karachentsev Hα program has covered hundreds of Local Volume dwarfs**; (c) **existing spectroscopy, including DESI fibers on or near the candidate**. This table defines the remaining novelty, the paper's framing, and the title/abstract counts — none of which are fixed before it exists.
3.2 **QC gates (hard cuts, the *only* rejection authority, reported):** unsolvable; ensemble ZP > 0.3 mag below nightly median; FWHM > 12 px; transparency loss > ~1 mag (ZMAG < 21.5). Applied uniformly in the pipeline over all campaigns (Dw, NGC 5238, NGC 5548); the disposition table emits the rejected frames and nights. **No night list is enumerated in this memo or the paper text.**
3.3 **Stacks:** SWarp/reproject weighted coadds per field per filter; weight ∝ 10^(0.8·(ZP−22.8))/σ_sky²; sky as a **constant per frame** (never mesh-based local background — it eats LSB flux).
3.4 **Depth, measured not asserted — then a detectability gate:** Román, Trujillo & Montes (2020) metric — 3σ in 10″×10″ random apertures — per field per filter (a *measured* metric, robust to the correlated pixel noise that resampling introduces; the μ≈27.5 photon-limit arithmetic is a consistency check, **not an attainability claim**). Certify with **synthetic-dwarf injection–recovery into the actual coadds** (grid of μ_0, r_e). Then build the **per-candidate detectability table: Legacy-measured (μ₀, r_e) vs the per-field 50%/90% recovery contour.** Candidates predicted undetectable at our depth (the discovery imaging reaches μ ≳ 28; we realistically reach 26.5–27.0) contribute **no vetting claim** — a non-detection shallower than the discovery surface brightness is consistent with both "real" and "artifact"; they appear in the disposition table only. The six L-only fields are expected to fail this gate; if so, the honest vetting count is 13 and the paper says so. Q1 (Hα) is unaffected — emission-line detection does not require continuum LSB depth — which is one more reason Hα carries the paper.
3.5 **Detection/measurement:** stacks binned 3×3, smoothed to ~8″; aperture photometry + Sérsic fits with **imfit** (preferred over GALFIT for LSB): μ_0, r_e, ellipticity; colors where R exists.
3.6 **Hα → flux limits first, SFR second:** continuum-subtract with scaled R stacks (13 fields with both); calibrate Hα flux via field stars. Required inputs before any flux is quoted: the actual Hα filter transmission curve (center, width — Cannon, via gate 0.2); [N II] contamination correction; correction for Hα flux contained in the R continuum band (over-subtraction). **Primary deliverable: Hα *flux* detections/upper limits.** SFR (Kennicutt & Evans 2012) is quoted only at an explicitly assumed fiducial distance per candidate (the parent catalog's presumed group association), with the D² scaling stated in the table header — the candidates have no distances, by construction. Add the stochastic-IMF caveat on Kennicutt–Evans at SFR ≲ 10⁻³ M☉ yr⁻¹. Vet every LSB/Hα detection against Herschel/IRAS 100 µm and WISE 12 µm cirrus maps.
3.7 **Photometry mode:** aperture + curve-of-growth corrections (photutils/sep). No PSF fitting — at 5.4″ FWHM, uncrowded fields, it buys nothing.

### Phase 4 — NGC 5548 (Q4)

4.1 **Aperture and sky — decided *after* Phase 0.3, host-aware:** the campaign's FWHM history is currently unknown (0 solved frames); no aperture is committed before the solved-frame FWHM distribution exists. Baseline: fixed metric aperture r = 5.4″ (10 px) **with sky measured well off the host galaxy** — the NGC 5548 disk extends beyond 30″, so an 8.1–10.8″ annulus sits *on* the host; use source-masked sky regions at r > 60″ (or a constant-sky model over the solved frame). Host flux in the aperture is constant only for constant PSF: (a) estimate the host fraction in the aperture from published NGC 5548 surface photometry (the AGN STORM host decomposition exists for exactly this); (b) propagate an explicit seeing-jitter host-leakage systematic into the error budget; (c) after Phase 0.3, **regress nightly magnitudes against FWHM, airmass, and sky level and report the coefficients** — if the FWHM term leaks >5 mmag over the observed seeing range, switch to r = 1.5×FWHM(night) with per-night growth-curve corrections to 4×FWHM. **The host fraction is quoted alongside F_var** — aperture-diluted F_var is not comparable to Xi et al. without it. ⚖ Memo 1's fixed aperture retained as baseline, now with the sky moved off the host and a quantitative trigger for the adaptive fallback.
4.2 **Differential ensemble:** 10–20 comparisons, 12 < m < 15.5, **|Δ(g−r)| < 0.4 of the ensemble median color** (the AGN's color is variable and non-stellar; the fitted color term handles the AGN–ensemble offset), Gaia RUWE < 1.4, clean DR3 variability flags; inverse-variance (Honeycutt/Broeg) weighting; iterate rms–mag diagram, drop >3× noise envelope. Absolute scale enters once via the ensemble's REFCAT2 tie.
4.3 **Nightly points:** sigma-clipped mean of intra-night frames. With N = 5–10 frames/night, per-night scatter/√N carries 25–35% uncertainty on the error bar itself — so the quoted nightly uncertainty comes from a **global rms–magnitude noise model evaluated per night**, χ²-validated on non-variable comparisons at the same magnitude (χ²_ν ≈ 1; rescale by √χ²_ν if needed and report the factor). ⚖ This merges Memo 1's PTC-based errors with Memo 2's empirical-scatter errors: PTC informs the per-frame arrays; the *quoted* nightly errors are empirical, model-smoothed, χ²-validated.
4.4 **Statistics only, no models with free timescales:** χ² vs constancy; F_var with Vaughan et al. (2003) uncertainty (quote the larger of the formula and the comparison-star null spread), **with the host dilution factor stated**; first-order structure function on 1–30 d with the **pair count per lag bin published** — all lags > 13 d come exclusively from cross-gap pairs (13 d + 8 d blocks), so truncate or flag bins with few pairs and cite the known SF biases for short gapped series (Emmanoulopoulos et al. 2010). celerite2 DRW only with τ fixed to literature prior, quoting σ̂ — never a fitted τ (τ ~ 100–200 d ≫ 33 d baseline). No Lomb-Scargle on the AGN, period.
4.5 **Deliverable — conditional, and band-honest.** Two gates before the section is written. **(i) Band physics:** if gate 0.2 identifies slot '6' as luminance, the bandpass contains Hα, Hβ, and [O III]; for NGC 5548 the BLR lines are a non-trivial fraction of band flux and reverberate on day–week lags, so this is **not** a continuum light curve — estimate the line-flux fraction in the adopted band from the 2024-06-05 grism visit and/or archival spectra, quote it as a systematic, present the product as "band-integrated photometry densifying and cross-checking the RM-season continuum," and state that short-lag SF shape and the PyCALI mapping to g inherit both the line contamination and the few-percent epoch-dependent bluer-when-brighter chromatism of an L↔g tie. This caveat lives in manuscript §3.4, not a footnote. **(ii) Value proposition:** build the coverage/precision table for ZTF g / ASAS-SN g / ATLAS o over the exact spring-2023 window — cadence, per-epoch precision, and bright-limit behavior at g ≈ 13.5–14 (ZTF/ATLAS are at/near saturation there; the RLMT edge, if any, is precision, not cadence — combined public coverage is near-daily). **If the table shows no densification or precision gain, execute the RNAAS fallback without regret — do not pad the ApJ paper.** Otherwise: PyCALI/PyROA intercalibration and overlay on the Xi et al. 2025 continuum. The 2024 grism visit = the line-fraction estimate + one figure, nothing more.
4.6 **Times as BJD_TDB** (astropy.time barycentric correction) for everything in the paper — headers are UTC JD.

### Phase 5 — NGC 5238 (Q3) and the variability census (Q5)

5.1 NGC 5238: same Phase 1–3 machinery; per-band deep stacks + continuum-subtracted Hα SF map. Night rejection is **threshold-driven via §3.2 only** — the measured nightly R ZP spreads form a continuum (0.00–0.57 mag on usable nights, 3.6–3.9 mag on the worst two), and any enumerated list drawn by eye fails a consistency audit (⚖ Memo 2's three-night drop list kept a night worse than one it dropped; retired).
5.2 Image-subtraction variability/transient search (HOTPANTS/ZOGY) across 21 epochs; check grism frames for usable spectra of the brightest knots.
5.3 Field-star period search (NGC 5238 and ≥10-night Dw fields only): astropy LombScargle, 0–5 c/d grid oversampled ≥10×, separate 5–150 c/d pass where per-frame photometry supports it; PDM + phase-fold verification; consistency across independent night blocks; FAP by ≥10⁴ bootstrap shuffles of the real time stamps (not Baluev alone — the window is clumpy). For any candidate f₀, test f₀ ± 1.0027 and ± 0.0027 c/d aliases; adjudicate by ΔBIC ≥ 6 or report both.
5.4 **Injection–recovery completeness map (mandatory):** sinusoids + eclipse templates injected into real comparison-star light curves on real time stamps — periods 0.05–30 d, amplitudes 2–200 mmag, 20 phases; report the 90%-recovery amplitude vs period vs magnitude. This converts non-detections into a publishable statement.
5.5 Detrending: frame-level regression against covariates (airmass, FWHM, x/y drift, sky) with ≤2 Sys-Rem-like components; **never** GP-detrend in time before a period search; fit trend + signal simultaneously where a secular trend is suspected. Multiband LS (VanderPlas & Ivezić 2015) with floating per-band offsets when merging L and R+Hα blocks.
5.6 Eclipse timing only if EBs emerge: Kwee–van Woerden for fully covered minima, template fits otherwise; period refinement to ~10⁻⁴ d is supportable; period-*change* claims are not.

| Failure mode | Consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate frames leak into analysis | Depth inflated ~3×, errors wrong by √N, referee catches count mismatch | Phase 0.1 dedup is the first gate; disposition table in paper |
| **Mispointed frames leak into a light curve** (e.g., NGC 5548 night 2023-03-25) | A fabricated epoch; every "verified" count distrusted | Pointing-validation columns in 0.1; counts frozen only after 0.3; Appendix C diagnosis |
| Filter '6'/slot letters unresolved (incl. Dw H/L/R and flat epochs) | Formally band-less photometry — desk rejection | Phase 0.2 dual-track identification incl. calibration frames; hard gate |

| StackPro noise assumed Poisson | χ², F_var, depth all wrong; variability claims unsafe | Phase 0.4 PTC from identified matched-mode pairs; empirical χ²-validated errors |
| **Host leakage through aperture/annulus tracking seeing** | Spurious mmag-level "variability"; F_var incomparable to literature | Sky off the host; host fraction from AGN STORM decomposition; FWHM/airmass/sky regression; adaptive-aperture trigger |
