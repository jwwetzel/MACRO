## 1. Executive summary

1. The paper is **multi-season, ~3-day-cadence Hα equivalent-width monitoring of bright emission-line stars with a 0.5 m slitless grism**, validated against BeSS and anchored to instrument-stability standards — a survey blind spot (all targets saturate ZTF/ASAS-SN/ATLAS/Gaia).
2. The science core is **λ Eri plus whichever targets the Step −1 BeSS emission-state check verifies as active**; Spica, Phecda, η Hya (HR 3454), θ Vir (HR 4963) are absorption/stability references, not Be science targets. **The BeSS check runs first, this week, before any per-target pipeline effort** (§4 Step −1).
3. All reduction starts from **`rawimage/`** (the `reduced/` tree is unaudited and demonstrably wrong in at least one case); errors are **empirical**, with the per-night error defined by **standard-star night-to-night scatter where standards exist (2025-12 onward)** and honestly labeled an *intra-night lower bound* elsewhere (§4 Step 10).
4. Supportable precision is quoted **per star in Å, not as a blanket percentage**: for strong emitters (|EW| ≳ 10 Å) the night-to-night floor is expected at ~0.1–0.5 Å; for the weak/shell/reference stars (net EW of a few Å) the same absolute floor is a 5–50% relative error and is stated as such. Era-to-era ties target ~1%; absolute spectrophotometry no better than 3–5%, and **for season 1 (2024-12→2025-04) only relative Tier-1 fluxes are quoted, systematic floor unknown** (no contemporaneous spectrophotometric standard exists; §4 Step 6). Period claims are limited to P < baseline/3, with night-block bootstrap significance and injection–recovery completeness maps.
5. Deliverables: nightly EW curves for ~10 stars over 2024-12→2026-07, outburst epochs to 1–3 d, a short-period (0.3–2 d) search **on the three targets with genuine multi-hour intra-night spans (Phecda, Spica, φ Leo)** with honest upper limits, disk-radius scalings (photospheric-corrected), and a methods demonstration that an undergraduate facility can patrol the survey-saturated bright sky.
6. **Venue framing (revised):** a **methods-plus-monitoring paper whose science yield scales with the verified-active count**. If Step −1 returns ≥4 BeSS-verified active emitters (ideally with a TESS-coincident event), pitch ApJ with the event as the lead; at 2–3 verified emitters the same paper goes to AJ/PASP without restructuring. The abstract is not drafted until Step −1 reports.

### 3.3 Sampling structure (Memo 2, chair-endorsed; short-tier rule added)

- Intra-night: 5–50 frame **stacking bursts** (spans mostly 9–73 min), not monitoring. **Effective independent N = nights (23–51 per target), not frames.** Every significance statement runs on nights or night blocks.
- Inter-night: median gaps 1–3 d; classic single-site 1-day comb at near-fixed UT; two-season targets add a ~1/220 d⁻¹ fine comb.
- Single-season baselines 86–181 d → **no period claims above baseline/3** (~30–60 d depending on target).
- **Short-tier admission rule (published as such):** a target enters the 0.3–2 d search only with ≥3 nights of >2 h intra-night span. Qualifying: Phecda, Spica, φ Leo (max spans 294–300 min). Not qualifying: λ Eri (max span 16.3 min).
- All timestamps converted to **BJD_TDB** (astropy, Winer site coordinates) before any period analysis; the ±few-minute heliocentric drift is a ~1%-of-cycle phase slew over a season at P ≈ 0.7 d — negligible for EW curves, not for phase-coherence tests.

### 3.4 Instrument reality — one canonical era table, keyed on READOUTM + geometry, used everywhere

Filter name ≠ era, and date ranges alone mislead: HaGrism raw lights run to **2025-03-21** (not 2024-11), and 49 hrg/lrg-labeled frames were taken with the era-A camera (2048², "1MHz High Sensitivity 16-bit", EGAIN 0). The pipeline therefore assigns era from **(READOUTM, NAXIS geometry, EGAIN)**, never from filter or date, and `make tables` emits the canonical era table the paper reprints:

- **A** — 1 MHz 16-bit, 2048²: 2024-04 → 2025-03 (mostly HaGrism/OGGrism, plus the 49 mislabeled hrg/lrg frames and the 2024-05-20 Vega ladder).
- **B** — ZWO driver, Mode0, EGAIN 0.2467 e⁻/ADU, pedestal 1000, thermally unregulated: 2025-01 → 2026-03.
- **C** — QHY driver, Fast, EGAIN 1.0 e⁻/ADU, −20 °C: 2026-03-22 →. Mid-era FITS-packaging change 2026-04-25 (§3.5).

Grism rig is an **IMX455-class full-frame CMOS (2×2 binned, 7.52 µm binned pixels; ~4788–4800 × 3194–3211 ROI depending on era), NOT the 4096² facility imager.** Treat the eras as three instruments with free offsets everywhere.

- **No grism plate solves, no ZMAG, no FWHM** (0 of ~25k). Catalog ZMAG-based calibration applies only to direct imaging. Header airmass unreliable (max 1425; 99 grism rows >3; 16 rows exptime ≤ 0) — recompute from RA/Dec + JD and QC out exptime ≤ 0. All airmass statistics in this document use recomputed values (clipped to physical values the lrg mean airmass is 1.36, not the header-derived 1.94 previously flagged as a red flag — that flag is withdrawn).
- **`camtemp` is NULL for every grism frame in the catalog.** The temperature series for the Step-11 regressor and the era-B "unregulated" characterization must come from a re-scrape of the FITS headers themselves (`CCD-TEMP` or driver equivalent) — Step 0 includes this scrape.
- **No grism-era calibration frames exist** (all cataloged bias/dark/flat end 2024-10, older-geometry cameras). The `reduced/` tree is unaudited and at least once wrong (Phecda same-night pair: raw peak ~6,600 ADU above bias vs "calibrated" 139 ADU above pedestal). **Reduce from raw.**
- Saturation: the manuscript makes **no sample-based claim**; Step 1 gates every frame on trace peak, and the paper publishes the full per-frame peak distribution from the QC log. (A 30-frame spot check suggested ladders are well tuned — peaks 1,000–26,000 ADU — but that anecdote does not appear in the paper.) Deliberate half/full exposure pairs (Phecda 0.13/0.25 s; 53 Boo 1.32/2.63 s; CQ UMa 4.29/8.57 s) plus the era-A Vega ladder give in-situ linearity curves **per era, including era A** — publish them.
- Photon noise is never the limit (per-frame trace SNR 500–14,000). **Scintillation is the per-frame floor** (~1.5% at 0.13 s → ~0.07% at 60 s; 0.05–0.2% per 50–160-frame epoch). Systematics dominate; the per-night error budget is set by continuum placement and night-to-night response drift, which is why per-night errors come from standards, not intra-night scatter (§4 Step 10).
- **StackPro clarification (chair's call, siding with Memo 1's verified readout modes over Memos 3/4's caveat):** 'High Gain StackPro' is the *facility imager's* mode; grism frames are Mode0/Fast/16-bit. The manuscript states this explicitly. The empirical-error policy stands regardless. Check READOUTM before using any *imaging* frame in Tier 3 calibration.
- One uncatalogued filter value **`lrgblue`** exists (1 raw frame, 2026-03-22 — the era-C boundary night, presumably a commissioning test). The Step-0 filter whitelist logs-and-excludes it explicitly.

### 3.5 Era-C FITS repackaging — catalog geometry is unreliable for era C (new, blocking)

**1,482 era-C hrg/lrg raw frames (11% of the hrg/lrg total) carry catalog `NAXIS1=8`, `NAXIS2≈3211`, file size ~8.9 MB** — consistent with image data living in a compressed or extension HDU rather than the primary. All are post-2026-04-25: the QHY-era writer changed FITS packaging mid-era. These frames include **343 of Vega's 367 era-C frames and 234 of θ Vir's** — i.e., the absolute-flux anchor and a primary standards series live almost entirely in the repackaged format.

Binding consequences:
1. **This week (with the Step −1 items):** open a sample of these files, characterize the HDU layout, and settle the hrg O₂ B-band coverage question (Step 4 note) in the same session.
2. The pipeline reader gets an **HDU-resolution layer** — never `fits.getdata` naively; select the image HDU by inspection of the actual layout, and hard-fail (never silently read an 8-pixel primary) on unrecognized packaging.
3. Step 0 treats **catalog NAXIS values as unreliable for era C**; the era-C inventory and era assignment are recomputed from file headers during the temperature re-scrape.

## 4. Analysis method — step by step

Language/env: Python, conda env `rlmt-checks` (`/opt/miniconda3/envs/rlmt-checks/bin/python`, astropy 6.1.7). Pipeline code home: `BeStar_Grism/pipeline/`, driven entirely by `rlmt-catalog.sqlite` queries. **`pipeline/make_tables.py` is the single source of every number in the paper (Table 1, era table, inventory); exact SQL published as Appendix C.**

**Step −1 — Sample verification and feasibility gates (THIS WEEK, before any per-target pipeline effort).**
(a) **BeSS emission-state check** for all ten targets (data already in the consortium `BeSS_Vis` repo; two-day task): current Hα state of λ Eri, 53 Boo, HD 70340, 69 Ori, 5 Cnc, plus confirmation that Phecda/φ Leo behave as assumed. The verified-active count fixes the venue (§1.6) and the §2 ranking *before* the abstract or figure list is frozen.
(b) **λ Eri injection–recovery** on the real timestamps through the short-tier machinery, run now, before a word is drafted. Expected outcome: confirmation that the 0.70 d search has near-zero completeness (§2 rank 4); the run's completeness map is what the paper cites when explaining the demotion.
(c) **Era-C FITS sample opening** + hrg bandpass check (§3.5; Step 4).

**Step 0 — Master frame table.**
Select `tree='rawimage'`, `imagetyp LIKE 'Light%'`, `error IS NULL`, grism filters (explicit whitelist hrg/lrg/HaGrism/OGGrism; `lrgblue` logged-and-excluded). Target matching: case-insensitive LIKE-prefix **plus alias table plus per-alias reconciliation report** (§3.2). Cone-match the 671 in-scope blank-target rows (RA/Dec, 0.5° radius). Tag each frame with instrument era from **READOUTM + geometry + EGAIN** (§3.4), with era-C geometry taken from re-scraped file headers, not the catalog (§3.5). Re-scrape FITS headers for `CCD-TEMP`/driver temperature (catalog `camtemp` is all-NULL). Recompute airmass from RA/Dec + JD (astropy `AltAz`, Winer site). Compute BJD_TDB per frame. Drop exptime ≤ 0.

**Step 1 — Frame QC gates** (all must pass before any series entry):
trace present (peak > 20σ above background — catches ghost frames); trace peak < 50,000 ADU (silent CMOS clipping flattens Hα cores); integrated trace S/N > 100; recomputed airmass < 2.5; trace spatial FWHM < 1.5× nightly median; |ΔFOCUSPOS| < ~100 from nightly median; background level/tilt within era norms. Log every rejection with reason; the QC log's per-frame trace-peak distribution is the paper's saturation statement (§3.4).

**Step 2 — Calibration frames (do this THIS MONTH).**
Request from the observatory: 50× bias + 50× dark at each (mode, gain, exptime) in use for era C; anything obtainable for era B — **the request explicitly asks whether the era-B (ZWO-driver) camera still exists in era-B configuration**. CMOS fixed pattern is stable enough that late-acquired darks recover most of it. Interim/era-B fallback: median-stack darkest on-sky frames per (era, exptime) as sky/dark surrogates; scale era-B dark structure using the hot-pixel population as a thermometer — **the surrogate-dark scheme gets a one-paragraph validation in the paper regardless of whether real era-B darks materialize**. Build the per-era **trace-illumination flat** (median of hundreds of continuum-normalized bright-star traces shifted to a common locus; residual PRNU < 0.3%). Measure RN and σ_dark ourselves; never quote vendor numbers.

**Step 3 — Trace + extraction** (`specreduce`, astropy-affiliated — chair's call, unanimous across memos: referees trust it and undergrads can run it; thin custom wrappers only where needed, including the §3.5 HDU-resolution reader layer).
Column-collapsed profile → centroid vs dispersion pixel → 2nd–3rd order polynomial trace, **fit per frame, never reused** (differential refraction shears the trace). Horne (1986) optimal extraction with empirical spatial profile and the era noise model var(e⁻) = S + B + n_pix(RN² + σ_dark·t), EGAIN per era. Background: per-column linear fit to two flanking strips ≥10 binned px off-trace; automated check for secondary traces (neighbor spectra) in the strips. Cross-check: fixed aperture ±2.5×FWHM_spatial; require <0.5% band-flux agreement or flag the epoch.

**Step 4 — Wavelength calibration (no slit, no arc).**
Dispersion (linear + quadratic) fitted **once per (filter, era)** from the Vega sets — era C from the 2026 series, **era A from the 2024-05-20 ladder** — vs CALSPEC `alpha_lyr_stis`; per frame, fit only a zero-point shift anchored on (a) telluric O₂ B band 6867 Å *if within the hrg bandpass — verified on an actual frame in Step −1c before this plan is frozen; if the hrg cutoff is blueward of 6867 Å, the hrg zero-point falls back to continuum cross-correlation alone and the paper says so*, (b) O₂ A band 7594 Å (lrg), (c) cross-correlation of continuum shape against resolution-matched CALSPEC template. Then a per-night flexure solution. **Report the achieved telluric-anchor rms in Å** — this number appears in the paper.

**Step 5 — Measure delivered resolution; it decides the scope.**
FWHM of the O₂ B band and of Hα absorption in Vega/Spica per night, per era. Expect R ~ few hundred (lrg) to 1000–2000 (hrg). Decision rule: if delivered FWHM at Hα < ~4 Å on hrg → attempt two-Gaussian V/R decomposition on λ Eri (+ ζ Tau); else V/R is out and the **asymmetry moment** substitutes.

**Step 6 — Response + telluric correction (chain rebuilt on verified overlaps).**
Sensitivity curve per (filter, era, season) anchored as follows:
- **Era C:** directly on Vega/CALSPEC (2026-04-16 → 06-25).
- **Era B ← C transfer:** via **η Hya (through 2026-05-12) and θ Vir (through 2026-06-13)** — the only stars whose grism series overlap Vega — back-propagated through era B to their series start (2025-12). **Spica is deleted from the transfer role** (it never overlaps Vega — last grism frame 2026-04-08 — and this document already bars it from ensemble calibration as a variable; it was never a legitimate response star). Phecda likewise cannot serve (ends 2025-04-18, a year before Vega begins, and is a null-test subject).
- **Era A:** anchored on the recovered **2024-05-20 Vega OGGrism/HaGrism exposure ladder** (80 frames, 0.0001–0.1 s, CALSPEC standard) — which simultaneously delivers the era-A in-situ linearity curve. This replaces the previous (false) premise that "Vega frames start 2026-04" and gives the A/B cross-calibration (Step 9) a real response solution on the A side.
- **Season 1 (2024-12 → 2025-04): no spectrophotometric standard exists** (η Hya/θ Vir start 2025-12). Season-1 fluxes are therefore **Tier-1 relative only, systematic floor unknown, stated as such in the text**; no absolute or pseudo-r flux claim is made for any season-1 event, and the EW/continuum degeneracy (Step 8 rationale) is acknowledged as unbroken for season-1 outbursts.
Telluric: empirical Vega spectra (mask + interpolate Hα), scaled by (airmass ratio)^0.55 for O₂ bands; `TelFit` fallback.

**Step 7 — Equivalent widths (primary science quantity — internally normalized, immune to transparency/ZMAG/gray-flat errors).**
**Fixed windows for the entire paper (chair's resolution of the three memos' differing windows):** EW integration **6520–6610 Å** (~±2000 km/s, generous for blended slitless profiles); continuum from a linear fit to **6480–6520 Å and 6620–6660 Å**. (Memo 4's red window 6600–6640 overlapped the line window — rejected; Memo 3's wide 6420–6500 blue window rejected in favor of Memo 1's, which stays clear of era-dependent response curvature; both flanks stay clear of the 6867 Å telluric band.) Per-frame EW errors via Vollmann & Eversberg (2006), then **overridden by the empirical two-floor model of Step 10**. Nightly value = median of ≥3 surviving frames (else flag night). Also compute per frame: asymmetry moment, line flux ratioed to local continuum, and a pseudo-r band flux (`synphot`) for the flux tier.
**Resolution-dependent EW bias control:** the fixed windows bias EW differently at lrg resolution (instrumental FWHM plausibly ≳15–20 Å smears Hα wings into the 6620–6660 flank) than at hrg or era A. Beyond the BeSS resolution-matched validation, the paper publishes an **hrg-degraded-to-lrg self-consistency test** on the stars holding both channels in quantity (Spica 372 hrg/356 lrg; θ Vir 189/179); λ Eri cannot provide this test (hrg-only) and its EWs are labeled hrg-channel values.
**Photospheric-absorption correction (required for disk physics):** measured EW = emission − photospheric absorption; for weak emitters the science quantity is a small difference of comparable numbers. Before any Grundstrom & Gies disk-radius conversion, subtract a resolution-matched photospheric-template EW (synthetic spectrum or BeSS-quiescent template per target). Raw and corrected EWs are both tabulated.

**Step 8 — Continuum/flux calibration in three tiers (Memo 1, adopted whole; season-1 restriction added).**
Tier 1 (relative, per era): nightly gray zero point via robust "night offset + star mean" ensemble solve (Honeycutt 1992) over all targets + standards that night — **Spica excluded from the solve** (ellipsoidal/β Cep variable); quiet stars admitted only after a χ² variability veto. Tier 2 (absolute): tie each era's response to CALSPEC Vega via the Step-6 chain; propagate as synthetic pseudo-r photometry — **available 2025-12 onward (late era B, era C) and for era A via the 2024-05-20 ladder; not available for season 1** (Step 6). Tier 3 (external check only): our own re-fit of the g/r/i direct images (114 r / 103 g / 59 i on-target) vs ATLAS-REFCAT2 (SEP/photutils, 2.5×FWHM apertures, 10 < r < 15, sigma-clipped ZP, per-filter/era color terms) — checks Tier 2 at few-percent, never drives it. **Why the flux tier exists (Memo 4's point): EW ≠ flux** — outbursts raise line *and* continuum, partially canceling in EW; the pseudo-r series breaks that degeneracy *where Tier 2 exists*; for season-1 events the text states the degeneracy is unbroken.

**Step 9 — Era cross-calibration.**
One offset per (star, band) from overlaps: A/B via HaGrism 2024-11→2025-03 (era A now response-anchored by the 2024-05-20 ladder, so this is a measurement, not assumption-on-assumption); B/C via stars spanning 2026-03-22. Verify offsets star-independent to <1%. Map mode-transition dates (including the 2026-04-25 packaging change) and show they don't coincide with any claimed EW break.

**Step 10 — Error calibration (revised: two floors, honestly separated).**
Intra-night: per era, for nights with ≥5 frames, fit multiplicative inflation *b* + additive jitter *s* so intra-night reduced χ² = 1; carry *s* into every model. **But intra-night scatter is blind to flexure, response, and telluric-state changes (frozen within an 8–73 min burst), so it is only a lower bound on the per-night error** — over the 90 Å window a continuum-placement error of just 0.1% already contributes ~0.09 Å. The per-night error the paper quotes is the **night-to-night scatter of the standards** (η Hya, θ Vir, Vega) — available 2025-12 onward. For epochs without standards coverage (season 1), per-night errors are labeled "intra-night lower bound" and carry the retro-standard systematic-floor term (§5.3). Precision is quoted **per star in Å**.

**Step 11 — Time-series analysis.**
Two series per target per channel (per-frame; nightly medians), **hrg and lrg kept separate, eras as separate instruments with free offsets — never merged before fitting.** All times BJD_TDB.
- *Slow tier (weeks–months):* GLS (`astropy.timeseries.LombScargle`, floating mean, nterms 1–2, 0–1.2 d⁻¹, oversample 10) + PDM (`PyAstronomy.pyTiming.pyPDM`) on nightly medians; FAP by **night-label bootstrap ≥10,000 draws**. λ Eri lives here.
- *Short tier (0.3–2 d; **Phecda, Spica, φ Leo only** — admission rule §3.3):* all frames + per-night nuisance offsets or night-block bootstrap; spectral window |W(f)|² published under every periodogram; Dawson & Fabrycky (2010) tests on ±1 d⁻¹ and ±0.0339 d⁻¹ alias partners.
- *Detrending decision rule:* never detrend-then-search. Joint fit {sinusoid + GP} vs {GP only} (`celerite2` SHO or Matérn-3/2; GP timescale bounded in [3×P_cand, season length]); detection = ΔBIC > 10 (+ Δln Z if nested sampling). **No claims for P > baseline/3.**
- *Systematics regressors:* EW vs recomputed airmass, re-scraped temperature, focuspos, instrument flag; any airmass-correlated "signal" is a slitless artifact. (The earlier "lrg mean airmass 1.94" red flag was an artifact of the corrupt header column — recomputed lrg mean 1.36; the flag is withdrawn, the regression stays.) Claimed periods must appear in both hrg and lrg at consistent phase **where both channels exist; the manuscript states explicitly that this gate is unavailable for λ Eri.**
- *Posteriors:* `emcee` (or `dynesty` for evidences) over {P, A, φ, offsets, jitter, GP}; quote 16/50/84; cross-check σ_P against σ·P²/(πAT√N) — a much-tighter posterior means the noise model is broken.
- *Injection–recovery (mandatory; λ Eri's runs first, in Step −1b):* sinusoids on real BJD_TDB timestamps; P 0.25–100 d log-spaced (~200 values) × 50 phases, through the **full** pipeline including detrend; amplitude grid starts at 0.25× night-to-night rms and **extends upward until the 90% completeness contour closes** (no fixed 4× cap — otherwise short-tier upper limits are open-ended); recovery = GLS peak within 1% of f_inj with night-bootstrap FAP < 1%. The 90% contour is the paper's sensitivity statement (sanity check: A_min ≈ 2σ_night√(z/N_nights), z ≈ 12 → ~1.5–2.5 σ_night).

**Step 12 — Event timing.**
Piecewise-linear / exponential-rise templates on nightly medians (BJD_TDB); onset epochs from χ² curvature (expect ~half the local night gap, 1–3 d). Monthly phase-coherence drift checks only for any short-tier candidate that survives Step 11's gates (a full O-C is not publishable — no eclipsing systems in the sample).

**Step 13 — External co-analysis.**
BeSS: beyond the Step −1 state check, pull all spectra of the ten targets 2024-11→2026-07 (consortium `BeSS_Vis` repo); degrade to measured resolution; remeasure EW with *identical windows*; the one-to-one plot is the validation figure. TESS: tesscut/halo photometry for every target's 2024–2026 sectors; hunt for RLMT Hα events inside sectors. AAVSO for the brightest. Physics layer: Grundstrom & Gies (2006) on **photospheric-corrected** EW→disk radius; BEDISK/BERAY forward models for λ Eri.
