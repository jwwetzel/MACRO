## Abstract (≈240 words)

Be stars brighter than V ≈ 8 saturate the wide-field time-domain surveys, so their Hα disks are followed mostly
by amateur spectroscopists contributing to the BeSS database. We present \NumNights{} nights of slitless-grism Hα
spectroscopy of the \NumStars{} Be stars that BeSS spectra show in emission during our 2024–2026 seasons, taken
with the 0.5 m Robert L. Mutel Telescope. A pre-registered check against BeSS found that none of the ten stars
first planned as the sample was in emission, so the sample is defined by independent spectra rather than by our
own. Equivalent widths are measured on fixed windows, with the dispersion fixed per grism and per mechanical state
of the instrument. The per-night error comes from two stability standards observed from 2025 December 5; earlier
seasons are reported but not used for claims of change. The night-to-night error floor is \NumFloor{} Å, and our
equivalent widths agree with resolution-matched BeSS measurements to \NumBessOffset{} ± \NumBessScatter{} Å over
\NumBessPairs{} contemporaneous pairs. \NumEventStars{} stars change by more than three times the standards' scatter
on consecutive nights; \EventSentence{}. A period search on nightly values, with injection–recovery run through
the detrending and a global false-alarm probability, finds \NumPeriods{} significant periods; at 90% completeness
it would have recovered sinusoids of \NumAninetyRange{} Å at periods of 5–20 d. \NumTessStars{} stars were observed
by TESS during our nights. On the stars where BeSS is sparse, a 0.5 m telescope with a slitless grism samples the
Hα line \NumCadenceRatio{} times more often than BeSS did over the same seasons.
