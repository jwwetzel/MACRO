Dwarf Galaxies + NGC 5548 — the case

Hα detections and flux upper limits for the 13 of 19 published DESI-Legacy dwarf-candidate fields that have Hα imaging, with deep-imaging VETTING restricted to candidates the per-field injection–recovery limits can actually reach; NGC 5238 — the archive's strongest time-series dataset — as the second pillar; and a band-integrated NGC 5548 nightly light curve intercalibrated with ZTF/ASAS-SN/ATLAS, conditional on a coverage/precision table showing a real gain over the public surveys. Explicitly not a lag, not a period, not a distance, and not a confirmation.

Where it is going: ApJ (NGC 5548 section conditional; pre-authorised RNAAS fallback)
