What this paper will claim

ApJ (NGC 5548 section conditional; pre-authorised RNAAS fallback)
Hα detections and flux upper limits for the 13 of 19 published DESI-Legacy dwarf-candidate fields that have Hα imaging, with deep-imaging VETTING restricted to candidates the per-field injection–recovery limits can actually reach; NGC 5238 — the archive's strongest time-series dataset — as the second pillar; and a band-integrated NGC 5548 nightly light curve intercalibrated with ZTF/ASAS-SN/ATLAS, conditional on a coverage/precision table showing a real gain over the public surveys. Explicitly not a lag, not a period, not a distance, and not a confirmation.

What's blocking

8 blocked tasks

Each entry states the reason and what would clear it. A blocker with no clearing condition is a decision nobody has made yet, and it is written as one.

The filter-slot dossier, science and calibration (DW-P02-filter-dossier, stage S2c)
Half the dossier is John Cannon's: the spring-2023 wheel configs, the Hα transmission curve, the W flats and Dw1643+07's provenance. He has not been contacted. Clears on that conversation — which the monsoon closure does not block.
Plate-solve everything (DW-P03-plate-solve, stage S1b)
NGC 5548 has zero WCS solutions, ever — 153 frames, none solved — and the aperture, sky and error-budget decisions of Phase 4 are all deferred until this closes. Clears when the S1b batch reaches these frames.
StackPro/High Gain noise model (DW-P04-noise-model, stage S2)
S2's PTC and detector tables were destroyed by the S0 table swap; χ², F_var and every depth number depend on this. Clears when S2 re-runs.
Flat strategy per campaign per filter (DW-P11-flats, stage S0b)
No flat for slot '6' exists at any epoch, and 'W' has a single 0.4 s frame. The acquisition rides the October request and is contingent on the retired cameras still existing; if they are gone, the Phase 1.1 decision trees stand as written.
Publish the band transformations (DW-P2-band-transformations, stage S4)
A transformation needs a band, and the band comes from the filter dossier. Clears with DW-P02-filter-dossier.
Hα fluxes and upper limits (DW-P36-halpha-limits, stage S4)
No flux can be quoted without the actual Hα filter transmission curve (centre, width) — Cannon holds it. Clears with DW-P02-filter-dossier.
Host-aware aperture and sky (DW-P41-aperture, stage S1b)
Explicitly deferred until Phase 0.3: with zero solved frames the campaign's seeing history is unknown, so no aperture may be committed. Clears with DW-P03-plate-solve.
Differential ensemble light curve (DW-P42-ensemble, stage S1b)
Needs WCS and the aperture decision above. Clears with DW-P03-plate-solve.
