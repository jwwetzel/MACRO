Status

ingesting

A candidate sixth project rather than a supplement to the five: the first frames scanned show a program dominated by contact binaries and eclipsing systems (W UMa, XY Leo, TU Boo, RW Com, CC Com, RZ Com, AW Vir, TX Cnc), plus HAT-P-12, a comet and an asteroid — only a small fraction overlaps the RLMT-era target lists. No committee strategy exists for it yet.

Current work: stage S0d makes the catalog multi-archive (archive_root + telescope columns, telescope-aware era keying so Rigel configurations can never share a camera era with RLMT ones, and a Rigel filename parser). The full target census — and therefore the real science case — lands when the transfer completes.

Why a separate archive root

Two reasons, both about not corrupting what already works.

Decision: the legacy data lives in legacy-archive/, never inside rlmt-archive/. That tree is a byte-verified mirror of the Linode testimages bucket (rclone check: 0 differences) and foreign files would break that equivalence permanently; and this is a different telescope, detector and filename convention, so merging them would let legacy frames leak into RLMT-era analyses. Consequence: the pipeline reads two roots and tags every frame with its origin.
