What this paper will claim

AJ — major revision open (reopened 2026-10-03; the venue was ApJ)
REOPENED 2026-10-03 — major revision; not complete. What the data deliver is one polar's colour–phase curve, a timing null and a release: ST LMi's g−r swings strongly through the bright phase with the same morphology in two instrument eras a year apart, state-tagged against the survey record; bright-phase edge timing bounds the change of the spin/spot-longitude period (not the orbital one); YZ Cnc contributes an excluded-amplitude superhump statement; VV Pup, EU UMa and AN UMa are a coverage audit. The draft's sentence that the bright-phase edge 'shows no offset' between bands is contradicted by its own released edges: g leads i on nearly every night both bands were timed. Whether that is astrophysical or an estimator bias is NOT decided (D3) — it is tested by per-band injection, and the paper reports whichever the test supports. Fifteen tasks (CV-R1 … CV-R15) block release.

Standing decisions

Rulings this project is already operating under. They are held in the plan ledger so a page regeneration cannot lose them.

Plan review of 2026-10-03 — the paper is not finished. Major revision (U3): the plan read 34 of 34 and the committee found four blockers in the abstract and conclusions. (1) The band-offset 'null' is a product of the error model — max(χ²ν, 1) on a transported budget doubled the error bars — and is re-tested with paired, scatter-based statistics. (2) The 'bias floor' is replaced by the signed matched-cell bias per band and era, and D3 (astrophysics or estimator bias?) is settled by injection, not by vote. (3) The O−C is refitted with per-band constants and night-level epochs. (4) The superhump 'measurement of absence' is restated as the amplitude range actually excluded. (5) The colour–phase result, which the abstract never quantified, becomes the lead. (6) The paper meets the literature, is cut to ≤ 18 pages with a ≤ 250-word abstract, and goes to AJ. (7) Nothing is citable until the chain is rebuilt FRESH at a clean commit and the macro diff is filed. (8) Authorship, ORCIDs, a DOI and an outside reader need James.

Per-frame verdicts from S2c, restricted to this project’s staged science frames. The measurement is source elongation, not the filter’s name — which is the point, since every strategy here carries an exclusion rule keyed on the name. The verdict column uses S2c’s own published rule — ≥80% dispersed is SPECTRA, ≥80% direct is images, anything else is MIXED, and only filters with at least 20 measured frames get a verdict at all — so the word means the same thing here as it does on the S2c page. The strength column matters: a verdict built from low-confidence frames is a weaker claim, and most dispersed verdicts in this archive are low. Frames S2c has not reached yet simply do not appear.
