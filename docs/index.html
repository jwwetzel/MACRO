How to read a page here

Every page on this site wears the same three layers, so you always know where you are.

  1. The top row names the shared pipeline and the six projects, and each entry carries its own condition — progress for a project, freshness for the pipeline.
  2. The second row is the five views inside one project: the case, the figures, the draft paper, the plan, and the evidence detail. A view that does not exist yet says so instead of linking an empty page.
  3. The left rail lists the questions of whatever you are looking at, in order. Scan it; click the one you doubt.

Nothing on this site is hand-maintained. The navigation is derived from the plan ledger, the provenance DAG and the pages' own section headings, which is why it cannot drift away from what is actually here.

The navigation, the questions and the figure groupings on this page are generated by macro_core.site (SITE v1.0 (2026-08-21)) from the plan ledger (macro_core.project_plan, PLAN v1.0 (2026-08-18)), the provenance DAG (macro_core.provenance, P v1.0 (2026-08-18)), the products databases, and the evidence pages' own section headings — never hand-maintained.
