SN 2023ixf: +5.4 to +50 d Validation and Limits Release — the case

A homogeneous, single-instrument nightly gri record of SN 2023ixf from +5.4 to +50 d, tied to PS1/REFCAT2, published as residuals against the world dataset with night-to-night variability limits and the saturation matrix that defines where it is trustworthy: the photometric pipeline's external validation, not an early-time physics paper. The unconditional clean start is +5.4 d and may be AT peak, so no 'rise' is claimed and 'early' has left the title; earlier epochs appear only in the saturated-frame inventory. The slot-'6' slitless grism series was triaged and NOT PROMOTED — zero extracted spectra, no wavelength source, no contamination test — and its frames go in the release; how much of that series is a grism series at all is the measurement in the panel immediately below. The one physics upside left is a flash-phase Hα excess from the (H − '[S II]') differential colour at three epochs, behind a predicted-excess gate. The model fit is dropped.

Where it is going: AJ/PASP — decided 2026-10-03 on Gate 0's pre-registered rule; the ApJ upside is closed

Q2

The screen, and where it comes from

SN-G0: FRESH

At what pixel value does this detector stop telling the truth — and does anyone have a measurement of it, or only a guess?

The deciding number
clip 3,496 ADU, measured
The decision it produced
The strategy's 2,800 ADU screen did not survive the linearity curve. At 2,800 ADU the response is already 2.3% low, so the screen moves down to 1,800 ADU. Every frame was re-judged from its stored peak (run_sn_gate0.py rescreen) without re-reading a pixel; the usable count in §5 is the consequence.

Q3

Gate 0b — the saturation census

SN-G0: FRESH

In which frames is the supernova itself measurable, and in which is it sitting on the detector's ceiling? The strategy's answer was an exposure-scaling argument. This is the pixels.

The deciding number
1,461 frames opened
The decision it produced
The clean broadband start is night 2023-05-23 (UT 05-24, +5.4 d), in all three broadband codes, exactly where the strategy predicted it by exposure scaling — and now by measurement. The three earlier epochs contain not one clean broadband frame between them: 2023-05-19 (+1.6 d) — 0 clean of 15 frames, 6 over the screen; 2023-05-20 (+2.5 d) — 0 clean of 12 frames, 12 over the screen; 2023-05-21 (+3.5 d) — 0 clean of 15 frames, 15 over the screen. The balance in each is undetermined: frames with no plate solution whose search box reaches the screen, which the census declines to call either way.

The flash-phase Hα record is not only the grism

Q6

Gate 0c — the grism triage

SN-G0: FRESH

Is the slitless series a genuine flash-phase spectral record — and does it clear the promotion bar the strategy pre-registered before any of this evidence existed?

The deciding number
83 measured spectra
The decision it produced
The series is genuine and it does not promote. 83 measured spectra on 15 nights, 3 of them inside the flash window; 0 extracted spectra. It has the nights — exactly the three the criterion asks for, with no margin — and it has none of the spectra. Nothing has been extracted, so nothing has been through the offset-trace contamination test, and no wavelength calibration source has been named. blocking clauses: extracted,contamination,wavelength; 0 filter-'6' flat frames exist in the archive, so the relative-flux-only policy stands

Q8

Astrometry for every frame

SN-PHOT: FRESH

Which broadband frames lacked a plate solution, and can each be solved?

The decision it produced
Every frame's WCS is re-fitted to REFCAT2 (header solutions are never used unrefined). Unsolved frames were solved by a translation vote under the campaign camera's measured CD matrix, then by triangle matching. The frames that remain unsolved carry a median of about a dozen 5σ detections — fewer than the eight calibration stars the photometry needs — so no solution could make them usable.

Q9

Filter identification and the MaxIm→pyscope crosswalk

SN-PHOT: FRESH

Which PS1 band does each filter code measure, and do the MaxIm campaign codes and the pyscope template codes agree?

The decision it produced

OPEN

Recover the narrowband transmission profiles

blocked

What would settle it: The actual H/O/'1' filter curves, from a manufacturer sheet or a monochromator scan. Only the ejecta-phase curve (SN-S6-halpha-curve) still needs them: the flash-phase estimator needs the filters' equivalent widths, which the zero-point ratios already give. Accept: a transmission curve on file by 2026-11-15, or the dated drop is recorded.

Blocked: External, and dated. A colour-term regression cannot measure a narrowband profile; the curve has to come from MACRO records or a member campus, and nobody holds it yet. Clears if it arrives by 2026-11-15; if it does not, this task and the ejecta-phase curve are dropped on that date by the ruling already made.

OPEN

Flash-phase Hα excess from the (H − '[S II]') colour

dropped

What would settle it: The SN's (H − '1') colour minus the field-star locus at the flash epochs, with errors, compared with synthetic photometry of published spectra through top-hats. A Type II at < +10 d has no [S II] emission, so that filter is pure continuum at the same exposures on the same stars. Accept: differential excess ± error at the three epochs, each beside its predicted scale (standing rule 2).

The strategy forbids running this first: SYNTHESIS §4: the flash-phase estimator sits "behind a predicted-excess gate" — the expected signal is computed before the measurement is attempted.

blocked
