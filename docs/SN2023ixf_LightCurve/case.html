SN 2023ixf Early Light Curve — the case

A homogeneous, single-instrument nightly gri and narrowband record of SN 2023ixf from +5.4 to +50 d, released with the saturation matrix that defines where it is trustworthy, validated against the published world dataset. The unconditional clean start is +5.4 d; '+1.6 d' appears only in the saturated-frame inventory. The upside — and the reason ApJ is on the table at all — is the slot-'6' slitless grism series from +3.5 d, which may be the archive's only unsaturated flash-phase Hα record, and an Hα-band evolution curve forward-modelled through a recovered transmission profile. How much of that series is in fact a grism series is MEASURED, not assumed: the panel immediately below is the measurement, and this upside is worth exactly what that panel says it is worth.

Where it is going: AJ/PASP base case; ApJ upside decided at week 3, post-Gate 0

The screen, and where it comes from

At what pixel value does this detector stop telling the truth — and does anyone have a measurement of it, or only a guess?

The deciding number
clip 3,496 ADU, measured
The decision it produced
The strategy's hand numbers survive contact with the measurement. 0.80 × 3,496 = 2,797 against a typed 2,800, and the suspect floor lands on 2,397 against a typed 2,400 — both inside 0.2%. The assumed ceiling was right. What has changed is that the screen is now a consequence of a query, so an S2 re-measurement moves it without anyone editing a document.

Q3

Gate 0b — the saturation census

SN-G0: STALE_UPSTREAM

In which frames is the supernova itself measurable, and in which is it sitting on the detector's ceiling? The strategy's answer was an exposure-scaling argument. This is the pixels.

The deciding number
1,461 frames opened
The decision it produced
The clean broadband start is night 2023-05-23 (UT 05-24, +5.4 d), in all three broadband codes, exactly where the strategy predicted it by exposure scaling — and now by measurement. The three earlier epochs contain not one clean broadband frame between them: 2023-05-19 (+1.6 d) — 0 clean of 15 frames, 6 over the screen; 2023-05-20 (+2.5 d) — 0 clean of 12 frames, 11 over the screen; 2023-05-21 (+3.5 d) — 0 clean of 15 frames, 15 over the screen. The balance in each is undetermined: frames with no plate solution whose search box reaches the screen, which the census declines to call either way.

Q4

The flash-phase Hα record is not only the grism

SN-G0: STALE_UPSTREAM

Is the slitless series really the archive's only unsaturated flash-phase narrowband record of this supernova?

The deciding number
+2.5 d, measured clean
The decision it produced
11 narrowband frames at +2.5 d are measured unsaturated on the supernova — three nights earlier than the strategy's stated clean narrowband start, and they are images, not spectra. The claim that the grism is the only unsaturated flash-phase Hα record in this archive is retracted here, on the evidence of the frames it was made about.

Q5

Does the astrometry verdict move?

SN-G0: STALE_UPSTREAM

The strategy inherited a NO-GO on this campaign's astrometry from the S1 experiment, whose candidate universe was contaminated by spectra it had excluded by filter LABEL. With the gate repaired to read S2c's per-frame measurement, does the verdict move?

The deciding number
NO-GO on the stratum
The decision it produced
The verdict does not move: the stratum stays NO-GO. 18/48 = 37.5% on the S1 stratum (unchanged verdict NO-GO); 553/630 = 87.8% of campaign broadband images carry a corroborated solution (GO).

Q6

Gate 0c — the grism triage

SN-G0: STALE_UPSTREAM

Is the slitless series a genuine flash-phase spectral record — and does it clear the promotion bar the strategy pre-registered before any of this evidence existed?

The deciding number
61 measured spectra
The decision it produced
The series is genuine and it does not promote. 61 measured spectra on 13 nights, 3 of them inside the flash window; 0 extracted spectra. It has the nights — exactly the three the criterion asks for, with no margin — and it has none of the spectra. Nothing has been extracted, so nothing has been through the offset-trace contamination test, and no wavelength calibration source has been named. blocking clauses: extracted,contamination,wavelength; 0 filter-'6' flat frames exist in the archive, so the relative-flux-only policy stands

Q7

The answers, with the numbers that decide them

SN-G0: STALE_UPSTREAM

Gate 0 exists to answer three questions and to set one posture. Each answer is stored beside the number it turns on, so a re-run that keeps the word while moving the number is visible in the table rather than only in a reader's memory.

The decision it produced
The venue posture stays at AJ/PASP. The strategy decided in advance that ApJ is taken only if Gate 0 promotes the grism or recovers the narrowband bandpass. Gate 0 did neither: zero contamination-tested spectra against a bar of 3, and zero recovered transmission curves against a bar of one. A posture that was decided before the evidence arrived, and that the evidence does not move, is a posture that held.
