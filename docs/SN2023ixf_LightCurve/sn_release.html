Decision

Every frame's WCS is re-fitted to REFCAT2 (header solutions are never used unrefined). Unsolved frames were solved by a translation vote under the campaign camera's measured CD matrix, then by triangle matching. The frames that remain unsolved carry a median of about a dozen 5σ detections — fewer than the eight calibration stars the photometry needs — so no solution could make them usable.
