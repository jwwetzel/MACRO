**Committee synthesis, 2026-08-16; revised same day after internal referee round.** Panel: Precision Photometry/Detector, Time-Series/Statistics, SN II Early-Time Physics, Literature/Archival Scout. Chaired by the Survey Scientist.
All numbers below were re-verified against `"/Volumes/OWC StudioStack HDD/DATA/ASTRO/rlmt-catalog.sqlite"` (table `obs`) after the internal referee demonstrated that the first draft's inventory was ~3x inflated by duplicates *within* `rawimage/`. ## 1. Executive summary

SN 2023ixf is the best-observed Type II SN in history; another gri light curve is an ApJ desk reject. The honest post-audit inventory is **1,052 unique light frames over 35 nights (2023-05-20 to 2023-07-07, ~48 d baseline), typically 5-7 single frames per band per night at roughly hourly cadence over 4-7 h spans** — not the 3,020-frame / 292-per-night campaign the first draft advertised (those were wholesale within-`rawimage` duplicates). Two structural facts now drive the plan:

1. **Clean broadband photometry cannot begin before ~+5.4 d** (UT May 24, the first 0.5-2 s frames). The +1.6 d (May 20, 256 s), +2.5 d (May 21, 32-64 s), and +3.5 d (May 22, 8-16 s broadband / 128 s narrowband) epochs put the SN far over the ~3.5 kADU high-gain clip by simple exposure scaling from the measured May-24 datum. With g-band peak at ~+8-9 d, the unsaturated gri *rise* is 2-3 nights. Worse: narrowband H exposures ramp back **up** to 128 s from May 28 through the peak weeks, so by zmag-based throughput scaling (~16x g-to-H) the flash-phase and peak-phase Halpha-band frames are expected ~3-5x over the clip. **There is a live risk that the only unsaturated flash-phase Halpha record in this archive is the slitless grism series.**
2. Therefore **Gate 0 — before filter identification, before any further planning — is: (a) the globally deduplicated manifest, (b) a pixel-level peak-ADU census of the SN in every frame of every filter, published as a saturation matrix (filter x night), and (c) the grism triage**, promoted from a side-track to a co-equal Gate 0 activity.

The candidate headline products, in order of upside: **(i) an 83-frame slitless-grism spectral series from +3.5 d** — if it survives a contamination-hardened triage, nightly flash-phase spectra from a student 0.5 m are genuinely new and carry the ApJ case; **(ii) an Halpha-band evolution curve over ~+5.4 to +49 d** (ejecta-phase Halpha emergence, *not* a flash-decay curve), presented as bandpass-forward-modeled and conditional on recovering the actual narrowband transmission curve; **(iii) night-to-night variability and bump limits over +5.4 to +50 d**, where continuous published coverage genuinely thins out — not "mmag intra-night limits during the rise," which the post-dedup cadence (single ~hourly frames, scintillation-limited at 5-10 mmag each) cannot support. All of it is validated by a 35-night gri curve tied to Pan-STARRS and overlaid on the published world dataset. The standing gates remain: empirical filter-bandpass identification (single-character MaxIm codes are unpublishable as-is) and the linearity audit (measured ~3,530-3,550 ADU clip). Shock-cooling/CSM model fits are included only as consistency demonstrations against published parameters, never as headline constraints (no clean t < 5.4 d data, no UV leverage).

**Venue posture, decided now:** the AJ/PASP methods + data-release framing is the **base case**; ApJ is the upside case, taken only if the grism promotes and/or the Halpha bandpass is recovered well enough for a physical curve. This inverts the first draft's posture, and the panel prices that in before drafting, not at referee stage.

## 3. Data assessment (verified numbers, post-dedup)

### 3.1 Frame inventory
- **Canonical campaign: 1,052 unique light frames** (3,018 catalog rows with `target_best='2023ixf'` in `rawimage/`, collapsing to 1,052 distinct (basename, jd) pairs), 2023-05-20 to 2023-07-07, **35 nights**, ~48 d baseline. Maximum night: **56 frames (night of 2023-05-28/29)**. Typical structure: **5-7 single frames per band per night, roughly hourly, over 4-7 h spans** — there are no multi-frame "visits"; that structure in the first draft was an artifact of duplication.
- **The duplicates live *inside* `rawimage/`**: wholesale copies of earlier nights under `rawimage/2023-07-07/` (351 rows), `2023-07-08/` (743), `2023-07-09/` (733), plus smaller pockets (e.g. `2023-06-13/`, 199) — e.g. `xwg1411c.fts.fz` (2023-05-21T06:58:06.719) exists under both `rawimage/2023-05-21/` and `rawimage/2023-07-07/` with identical headers. `external/` holds exactly 1,052 frames (one copy of each unique frame — a useful cross-check) and `mjc/` holds 4; there is no `macalester` tree. **Dedup must be global on (basename, jd) — including within `rawimage` — before any statistic.**
- **[RESOLUTION — campaign start, revised after referee round]** The SN is present in frames labeled `NGC5457` on **2023-05-20 09:42 UT** (256 s G/R/X — **no H filter that night**) and `M101` on **2023-05-21** (32 s G/R/I + 64 s H/O/"1"), i.e. first epoch ~ +1.6 d post-explosion (t0 ~ MJD 60082.75-60082.83; Hosseinzadeh+23, Li+24). **These frames, and the May-22 8-16 s broadband / 128 s narrowband frames, are expected saturated on the SN** (exposure scaling from the measured May-24 datum — 2 s g -> 3,222 ADU peak against a ~3.5 kADU clip — puts 32 s at ~25-30 kADU-equivalent and 8-16 s at ~10 kADU-equivalent). They are folded in **only** as (a) the saturated-frame inventory and (b) standard-star calibration ladders. **"+1.6 d" appears in the paper only in the saturated-frame inventory. The unconditional clean start is +5.4 d (UT May 24).** The Gate 0 census (Sec. 4, Step 0b) is the arbiter for every frame.

### 3.3 Detector (measured, not assumed)
- All SN-era frames `READOUTM='High Gain'`, `EGAIN=1.054 e-/ADU`, 1x1 binning, GSense4040-class CMOS. **[RESOLUTION]** Seats 2/4 warned of StackPro on-camera-stacking pathologies; Seats 1/3 verified the headers: **this dataset is plain High Gain, not StackPro** — conventional noise properties apply. The StackPro caveat is dropped for this paper (keep it for other RLMT projects).
- **Hard clip at ~3,530-3,550 ADU (~3.7 ke- full well of the high-gain channel), not 65,535.** Median frame pedestal ~93 ADU -> usable dynamic range ~3.4 kADU. Measured consequence: on 2023-05-24 the SN reaches 891 ADU in 0.5 s g but **3,222 ADU in a good-seeing 2 s g frame (xwg1441f) — inside the clip.** Seeing-dependent saturation of the target itself exists near maximum light even in the short-exposure regime.
- **Decision rule (binding):** reject any SN or comparison-star measurement with peak pixel > **2,800 ADU** (~80% of clip); flag 2,400-2,800 ADU as suspect pending the empirical linearity curve (Sec. 5.2). The Gate 0 census applies this rule to the SN in every frame and publishes the result.

### 3.4 Templates
- **Pre-explosion (-14 d): 2023-05-05**, NGC5457, **8x256 s** each in G, R, H, O, X (not 16x as first drafted). **Caution (Seat 3, verified in catalog): FWHM 14-16 px in G/R/X vs ~5 px in H/O — possibly trailed/defocused. Inspect before trusting; may be usable only in the narrowbands.** No pre-explosion I template exists (coordinate-cone check +/-0.25 deg confirms no other pre-discovery coverage).
- **Post-fade:** 2023-11-28 (4x64 s L), 2024-03-12 (L, poor), **2024-05-19 (30x90 s g + 30x90 s r, unsolved — the deepest post-fade g/r material; solve and stack)**, 2026-04-02 (+1049 d): g/r/i, 120-250 s, FWHM ~2 arcsec, but only **2-3 reduced frames per band** (g: 3, r: 2, i: 2; reduced-frame zmag 23.0/22.2/21.2) — marginal for artifact rejection on its own.
- **[RESOLUTION — primary template, revised after referee round]** Residual SN flux in 2026 (r > 21) contaminates bright-phase (11-14 mag) photometry at <=1e-3 — negligible; that part stands. But two new constraints bind: (a) **2-3 frames per band cannot support robust artifact rejection** under a subtraction the headline figures depend on, and (b) the 2026 templates are **pyscope-era g/r/i**, whose equivalence to the MaxIm G/R/I codes is exactly what Step 1 must first establish — cross-bandpass subtraction on an Halpha-bright arm leaves color-dependent residuals at the SN position. Ruling: **the MaxIm-to-pyscope filter crosswalk is a prerequisite for template adoption**, not just an appendix item; primary broadband templates = **2024-05-19 deep g/r stacks** (30 frames/band) once solved and PSF-vetted, with 2026-04-02 as the i-band template and the g/r cross-check; bandpass-mismatch residuals quantified with the 20-field-star test **run on the spiral arm itself** (Sec. 5.4); 2023-05-05 remains (i) a cross-check where G/R pass inspection and (ii) **primary for narrowband H/O** (PSF is fine; no post-fade narrowband exists). For any measurement with SN fainter than ~16.5 mag, quantify residual SN flux in post-fade templates via PS1-stack cross-subtraction and propagate it as a systematic — state the choice explicitly in the paper.

### 3.5 Calibration frames
One in-window flat epoch (2023-07-01, 11 filters, 0.1-2.3 s) + 120 in-window darks; CCD-TEMP -16.8 to -19.6 C vs -20 C setpoint. **No filter-6 (grism) flats exist** — grism policy in Step 0c. The flat epoch sits at the campaign *end*, applied backward six weeks to a CMOS: the repeatability test is split early/late to bound temporal drift (Sec. 5.3). Risks and mitigations in Sec. 6.

## 4. Analysis method — step by step

Environment: conda `rlmt-checks` (`/opt/miniconda3/envs/rlmt-checks/bin/python`, astropy 6.1.7). Add: photutils, astrometry.net (local solve-field), HOTPANTS **and** sfft (pick per Sec. 5.4 validation), specreduce, emcee/numpyro, celerite2, astroquery. All code in `SN2023ixf_LightCurve/scripts/`, outputs in `data/`.

**Step 0 — GATE 0 (week 1; everything else is downstream of this).** Three parallel activities, all blocking:

**0a. Manifest freeze.** Dedup on (basename, jd) **globally — including within `rawimage/`** (the July 7/8/9 and Jun-13 directories hold wholesale copies of earlier nights); `rawimage` copy wins for path canonicalization; cross-check the unique count against `external/` (must equal 1,052, + the 4 `mjc` frames). Write `data/manifest.csv` with one row per unique frame: path, jd, filter code, exptime, fwhm, zmag, pltsolvd, night_id (local-noon split). Include the 2023-05-20 NGC5457 and 2023-05-21/22 M101 frames. Route filter "6" to the grism track; drop L from photometry. Every downstream script — and the README's campaign statistics — reads only the manifest.

**0b. Full-campaign SN saturation census.** Pixel-level peak-ADU measurement of the SN in **every** direct-imaging frame, every filter (rough WCS from pointing where unsolved; small-stamp maximum at the SN position). Deliverable: the **saturation matrix (filter x night)** with the Sec. 3.3 screen applied — published as a figure and as flags in the released table. This decides: which (if any) 128 s H epochs are clean, the true clean start per band, and whether Q2 survives as a physical product. No phase claim appears anywhere in the paper except as read off this matrix.

**0c. Grism triage (timeboxed: 2 weeks).** Extract the +3.5 to +10 d nights (at most 3-4: May 22/24/25/27) with specreduce. Requirements hardened after the referee round: **(i) trace ID** — first *verify* from the manifest that paired or near-in-time direct images exist for each grism night (do not assume); **(ii) wavelength solution** — from a **named, dated** calibration source: search the archive for 2023 May-June grism standards (query `filter='6'` on standard-star fields); if none exist, fall back to self-calibration on the SN's own Halpha + known field-star features, and say so in the paper; **(iii) contamination test** — the field is an M101 spiral arm, and slitless dispersion superposes the spectra of every nearby star and H II region on the SN trace: extract **parallel offset traces** above/below the SN trace and require the candidate flash features to be absent there — ambient H II-region Halpha must not be promotable; **(iv) flux calibration** — no filter-6 flats exist: **relative, continuum-normalized spectrophotometry only**; no absolute fluxes. Promotion criterion: Halpha emission with electron-scattering wings at SNR > 10 across >=3 nights **and** a passed offset-trace contamination test -> headline figure + dedicated section. Otherwise: one appendix paragraph, no further effort. Hand the full 83-frame set to the spectroscopy pipeline either way.

**Step 1 — Filter identification (blocking; week 1-2, parallel with Gate 0).**
(a) Pull MACRO 2023 filter-wheel configuration records **and purchase records / manufacturer transmission sheets — required for the narrowbands (see Step 6)**. (b) Empirically for broadbands: cross-match ~100 PS1/ATLAS-REFCAT2 stars per frame; regress instrumental ZP residual vs catalog g-i (or g-r) per filter code. Sloan g'r'i' vs Johnson BVR produces unmistakable ~0.2-0.3 mag/mag color-term differences. (c) Confirm the code crosswalk to pyscope-era lowercase names (58 crossover rows already support "1" -> SII) — **this crosswalk gates template adoption (Sec. 3.4), not just the appendix table.** (d) Explicit scope limit, conceded in the paper: color-term regression identifies broadband filters; **it cannot measure a narrowband transmission profile** (center, width, cut-on/cut-off) — that requires the manufacturer curve or a scan (Step 6). Deliverable: the filter-mapping appendix table. **Nothing photometric is publishable before this lands.**

**Step 2 — Linearity audit (blocking; week 1-2).**
Derive the linearity curve empirically from the exposure ladder we already own: 0.5 s vs 2 s pairs on the same stars, May 24-25 (4x flux ratio; fit fractional deviation vs peak ADU), cross-checked against twilight-flat ramps. Fix the clip value per the measured ~3,530-3,550 ADU ceiling. Publish the curve. The Gate 0 census (0b) supplies the SN-side screen; this step supplies the star-side screen and the linearity correction below 2,800 ADU.

**Step 3 — (absorbed into Gate 0c.)**

**Step 4 — Re-solve and calibrate.**
Re-solve the ~177 unsolved broadband frames with astrometry.net. Per frame: measure 30-80 ATLAS-REFCAT2 stars (12 < r < 16, 0.2 < g-r < 1.2, peak < 2,800 ADU, isolated: no neighbor within 3x FWHM above 5% flux). Per-frame ZP = inverse-variance-weighted 3-sigma-clipped mean. Fit one campaign-level color term per filter + nightly extinction k*X, **plus a second-order (color x airmass) extinction term** — with X up to 2.7 and a very blue source it enters at the 10-20 mmag level, material against a 10 mmag internal target. Header `ZMAG` (PinPoint, as few as 13 stars, spans 1.4 mag in g) is used **only** as a cloud/QC flag (reject frames > 0.5 mag below per-filter campaign mode) — never as calibration. Frame rejection: FWHM > 6 px, calibration RMS > 0.05 mag, < 8 usable standards. **Exposure-regime tie:** in 0.5-2 s frames only r ~ 12-14.5 stars have usable S/N, while 64-128 s narrowband frames clip stars brighter than r ~ 15.5 — the standard ensembles are disjoint. Demonstrate the tie explicitly with the intermediate ladder we own: the 8-16 s (May 22) and 32 s (May 21) broadband frames — SN-saturated but star-clean — plus the 4-8 s I frames. Report magnitudes in the **RLMT natural system tied to PS1**, publishing transformation coefficients; compute per-epoch S-corrections by synthetic photometry of public flux-calibrated 2023ixf spectra (WISeREP) through measured/candidate bandpasses (scope per Sec. 5.6).

**Step 5 — Photometry (two regimes; single-frame epochs).**
- **Bright phase (+5.4 d - mid-June, SN brighter than ~14):** aperture photometry (photutils), r = 1.5x FWHM, sky annulus 4-6x FWHM sigma-clipped. Host under an 11-13 mag point source is sub-percent.
- **Fading phase (SN fainter than ~16.5), all narrowband, all late epochs:** template subtraction (HOTPANTS or sfft; Sec. 5.4 picks one; templates per Sec. 3.4) then forced PSF photometry at the fixed stacked-image position (PSF from >=15 field stars). Kernel validated on 20 field stars **on the spiral arm**; require subtraction residual RMS < 3x sky.
- **Overlap zone:** show aperture and subtracted photometry agree to < 0.02 mag above mag 13; quote the difference as a systematic. **[RESOLUTION]** Seat 4 wanted subtraction everywhere; Seats 1/3 showed it is unnecessary (and adds kernel noise) in the bright phase. Ruling stands: two-regime approach with the published overlap test.
- **Epoch structure:** post-dedup there are **no multi-frame visits** — 5-7 single ~hourly frames per band per night. Each frame is one epoch; the nightly weighted mean (scatter-based error) is the unit for model fits and night-to-night statistics. All "visit collapse" machinery from the first draft is deleted. Release per-frame and nightly tables.

**Step 6 — Halpha-band curve (conditional track; scope set by Gate 0b + the transmission curve).**
Prerequisite: the actual H (and O, "1") transmission curves — manufacturer data sheet via MACRO records, or a monochromator scan of the physical filter at a member campus. **If neither is obtainable, Q2 is demoted to a methods demonstration and the physical-interpretation section is cut.** Method, given a curve (or a family of candidates): **forward-model** the narrowband flux by synthetic photometry of the public flux-calibrated spectral series (WISeREP) through the bandpass, and present the RLMT curve *against* that model — explicitly bandpass-modeled, never as a raw "Halpha light curve." Two first-order effects the model must carry: (i) the line broadens from few x 1,000 km/s electron-scattering wings to +/-8,000 km/s ejecta Halpha (~350 A full width) — wider than any plausible interference filter, so F(H) declines with broadening even at constant line flux; (ii) **r contains Halpha itself**, so the continuum proxy is built from g/i interpolation (the r-based F(H) - k*F(r) version, k from field stars, is retained as a cross-check only) to avoid a phase-dependent self-subtraction bias. Narrowband template subtraction uses the 2023-05-05 H/O stacks; the H-to-broadband tie uses the Step 4 exposure ladder. Concede the partial circularity in the text (interpretation leans on the published spectra the product complements) and sell what is genuinely new: homogeneous, single-instrument, nightly narrowband coverage. Deliverable: nightly H-band flux + error over the census-approved epochs (~+5.4-6.4 d, then post-recovery), with the Jacobson-Galan+23 spectroscopic epochs marked. Repeat for [O III]/[S II] if S/N permits; cut any band whose ZP systematics exceed the signal.

**Step 7 — Model consistency fits (per Q5 ruling).**
Joint multi-band flux fits (nightly epochs, flux not magnitudes) of Morag+23 / Sapir-Waxman and Martinez+24 CSM grids to the census-approved clean epochs (>= +5.4 d), t0 and distance fixed to literature (provenance stated in text); report parameter consistency and model-to-model shifts, presented as parametrization. GP detrend (Matern-3/2, **length-scale prior floor >= 2 d**) for the residual analysis; cross-check with a nightly-median spline; refit GP + signal simultaneously (celerite2) for any candidate.

**Step 8 — Variability limits (reframed per Q3).**
Lomb-Scargle per VanderPlas 2018: with ~hourly single frames the pseudo-Nyquist limit is ~10-12 /d; **set f_max ~ 10 /d** and state that all quoted limits are injection-recovery-defined, not periodogram-defined. delta-f = 1/(10 x ~44 d clean baseline). **Publish the computed spectral window function** (1 d comb + sidelobes + structure from the real gap list in Sec. 3.2 — do not pre-describe it). FAP by **whole-night bootstrap** (never frame shuffles). Detection bar: FAP < 0.1% AND independently present in >=2 bands AND survives half-split. Otherwise: **injection-recovery** limits — sinusoids (P 0.1-10 d) and 0.5-2 d Gaussian bumps injected at the real time stamps with the empirical noise model; report the 90%-recovery amplitude-vs-period curve on an honest grid (**~10-100 mmag intra-night, ~5-20 mmag night-to-night**). Position against the published record honestly: the rise is already covered at minute cadence by Li+24 and the citizen-science network; our added value is homogeneous nightly coverage through peak/decline to +50 d. This figure is the quantitative Q3 result.

**Step 9 — Late-time + limits.**
Template-subtract 2024-05-19 and 2026-04-02 stacks against PS1; expect non-detections; quote per-night stacked 5-sigma limits from injection into difference images near the SN position (~19.5-20.5 r single-frame; deeper stacked).

**Step 10 — Release.**
Machine-readable photometry table (per-frame + nightly, with saturation-census flags and natural-system + PS1-tied columns), H-band table, grism spectra if promoted, the saturation matrix, pipeline on GitHub, archive on Zenodo. **Harmonize the README from the manifest** (first draft and README disagreed on start date and totals — 05-22 vs 05-20/21, 3,018 vs 3,020; the manifest is the single source of truth).

## 5. Calibration & validation plan

1. **Ensemble ZP:** REFCAT2 as in Step 4; per-frame ZP uncertainty target 3-5 mmag; constant-field-star reduced chi-squared ~ 1 is the published proof the error model is honest.
2. **Linearity:** Step 2 curve; residual < 5 mmag below 2,400 ADU or the screen threshold tightens.
3. **Flats:** single 2023-07-01 epoch (campaign end, applied backward six weeks to a CMOS) + 0.1 s rolling-shutter flat risk -> measure achieved flatness empirically via comparison-star repeatability vs (x,y) across dither positions, **split into early-campaign and late-campaign halves to bound temporal drift**; if the residual surface > ~5 mmag, fit and remove a 2D-quadratic delta-flat (per half if they differ). Darks matched to +/-1 C for the 64-512 s narrowband/template/grism frames (irrelevant at 0.5-8 s). One-time i-band fringing check on a 256 s frame. **Grism: no filter-6 flats exist; relative spectrophotometry only (Step 0c).**
4. **Subtraction engine:** run HOTPANTS and sfft on the same 5-night validation set; adopt whichever gives lower field-star forced-photometry scatter **measured on arm-region stars**; report the choice. Quantify cross-bandpass (MaxIm code vs pyscope g/r/i) subtraction residuals at 20 arm positions before adopting any post-fade template (Sec. 3.4).
5. **Error budget (per single frame, post-dedup cadence):** Poisson ~ 7 mmag (2 s g at g ~ 11.2); scintillation (Young; D=0.5 m, X=1.4, h=1,515 m) ~ 10/5/2.5 mmag at 0.5/2/8 s — dominant in every short frame; read noise negligible above 12th mag. **Nightly epoch = weighted mean of 5-7 quasi-independent frames: statistical floor ~ 2-4 mmag, plus 3-5 mmag systematics each from ZP, flat/position, color-term, linearity -> realistic nightly internal precision ~ 5-10 mmag; intra-night sensitivity is a few tens of mmag, not mmag.** Targets: 10 mmag per nightly gri epoch internal, 20 mmag absolute to PS1, 20-30 mmag narrowband-subtracted. No "millimag" claims anywhere in the abstract.
6. **External validation (the centerpiece, with honest scope):** overlay RLMT on ZTF forced photometry, ATLAS, ASAS-SN, AAVSO AID, Hosseinzadeh+23 and Li+24 MRT tables, Chen+24 (Mephisto/Twin-50 cm — the same-aperture comparison the referee will make); interpolate to our epochs; report per-band mean offset + RMS. **The <= 0.03 mag systematic claim applies only to epochs where the SN color lies inside the stellar training range (0.2 < g-r < 1.2) — for 2023ixf that is the later plateau, not days 2-8.** For bluer epochs the color transformation is an extrapolation onto a hot continuum with emission lines: bound it by synthetic photometry of WISeREP spectra through the candidate-bandpass family and shade that envelope on Fig. 6; where the bound is too wide to be meaningful, truncate the comparison rather than defend it in a response letter. S-corrections are possible only to the extent bandpasses are measured (Steps 1/6); state clearly where only linear color terms were possible. Resolve any residual offset before submission, not in the response letter.
